// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Madison plumbing shops, answered.

[01]

What details does AVA get from a frozen-pipe caller?

Address, whether water is currently flowing, whether the main is shut off, what part of the house is affected, and access notes — captured in under two minutes and routed to your on-call phone as a structured SMS, not a rambling voicemail.

[02]

Can AVA tell a landlord call from a tenant call?

Yes, and it matters on the isthmus: AVA captures the caller's role, the property address, and who authorizes work, so your plumber is not standing on a Willy Street porch waiting for an owner callback that should have happened at intake.

[03]

Do you cover the whole county or just the city?

Wherever you run: the isthmus, Monona, McFarland, Fitchburg, Verona, Waunakee, Stoughton. Your boundary list is enforced on every call so dispatch never chases an address you will not drive to.

[04]

What if the caller has sewage backing up?

Backups get flagged urgent automatically — health hazard language in the intake does that — and routed to your emergency rotation with photos requested by text where that helps your crew arrive ready.
