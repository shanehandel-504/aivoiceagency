Questions owners ask

Straight answers.

What does AVA do when your phone rings?

AVA answers on the first ring, day or night. It talks with the caller, gets their name, number, address and what they need, and puts the job on your calendar. The caller gets a text with the time. You get a text and an email with the job.

Can AVA book jobs on my calendar?

Yes. We connect AVA to the calendar you already use. On the call, AVA offers open times, books the one the caller picks, and texts them the details. If you use a CRM, the job can go there too.

What happens when a call is urgent?

You tell us what counts as urgent: a burst pipe, no heat in January, a patient in pain. When AVA hears it, AVA puts the caller through to your cell and tells you who it is and what is wrong before you say hello. You get a text and an email with the details too. The live transfer call above shows it.

Can AVA work with my existing phone number?

Yes. Your customers call the same number they always have. You choose what AVA answers: every call, only after hours, or only when your team is already on the phone.

Does AVA replace my receptionist or dispatcher?

No. AVA picks up the calls your people can’t get to: nights, weekends, and when two lines ring at once. Your team keeps doing the work only people can do.

What happens when AVA can't handle a call?

You pick what happens. AVA can send the call to your cell, take a message and text it to you, or set up a callback. You can name callers who always go straight to you, and you can pause AVA any time.
