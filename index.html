// 01 ── CAPABILITIES

What AVA does.

[01]

Inbound Answering

Answers on the first ring, every time. Understands why they called, asks the next right question, captures details, decides what happens next — book, qualify, route, text, escalate, or log. Uses your business rules, not a generic script.

[02]

Outbound + Speed-to-Lead Flagship

The second a lead hits your form, AVA calls — in seconds, while they still want to talk. AVA qualifies, books, and writes to CRM. Custom-built, not a canned dialer. Most phone rooms can't move this fast.

[03]

Live Warm Transfer Flagship

A lead fills out your form. AVA calls, qualifies, and live-transfers them straight to your closer while they're hot — or books a same-day appointment if your reps are slammed. The warm handoff that beats the no-show.

[04]

Booking + Routing

Warm-transfer, route by department, escalate VIPs, overflow at peak. Books appointments straight into your calendar. Right call, right person, instantly.

[05]

Missed-Call Recovery

Voicemail is where revenue goes cold. Instant text-back and callback — AVA qualifies, books, notifies — before the lead calls a competitor.

[06]

CRM + Workflow Writeback

The call ends. The record is already clean. Writes structured data into GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, calendars, inboxes. Contact created or updated, source captured, tags, task or appointment, summary attached, internal alert sent.

[07]

Custom Voices

Any voice. Any tone. Any language. Male or female — premium synthetic, or your own cloned voice (even the owner's). Your brand should sound like your brand.

[08]

24/7 + Multilingual

Every shift, every language your market needs. Nights, weekends, holidays, overflow — and switches language mid-call when the caller does.

[09]

Receptionist Layer

A receptionist when needed. An operating system when it matters. Greets, answers FAQs, takes messages, transfers, books — but the value is the qualification, routing, and writeback behind the voice.

Seconds. Speed-to-lead wins. The second a lead hits your form, AVA calls — while they still want to talk. Most phone rooms can't move that fast. AVA never misses the window.
