    Common questions.

    Will it sound like a robot?
    Tap play above — that's AVA's real voice. Decide with your ears. Any voice, any tone, any language is available, including your own cloned voice.
    What happens after hours?
    AVA answers. Nights, weekends and holidays, AVA picks up in one ring, books the appointment, texts the customer a confirmation, and flags emergencies to your cell — while the competition's line rolls to voicemail.
    Do I keep my number?
    Yes. AVA answers your existing line — nothing changes for your customers. They call the same number they always have; AVA just makes sure it's always answered.
    What does it cost?
    $497 per month plus a $497 one-time setup — that's the Starter System, done-for-you. Growth is $997 per month plus setup. Operations builds start around $1,997 per month, scoped on your call volume. Voice minutes are billed by usage. No contracts, cancel anytime.
    How fast is setup?
    Days, not months. We build AVA around your call flow and handoff rules, you test AVA on a real call, then AVA goes live on your line. You don't touch anything technical.
    What if AVA can't handle a call?
    The Ripcord. A VIP caller, an emergency, or certain trigger words, and AVA hands the call straight to your cell. You can pause AVA or take over any call, anytime.
