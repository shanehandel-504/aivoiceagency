The questions operators ask

Straight answers.

What does AVA do when your phone rings?

AVA picks up in one ring, any hour of the day. It greets the caller in your business's voice, listens to what they need, and pulls out the details that matter — name, number, address, what's wrong, and how urgent it is. Then it does the receptionist's real job: it books the appointment into your calendar or routes the call by your rules. The caller gets a confirmation text. You get a summary the moment it lands.

Can AVA book jobs on my calendar?

Yes — that's the point of the whole machine. During setup we connect AVA to the calendar you already run your day from. On a call, AVA offers real open slots, holds the appointment, writes the job into your CRM if you use one, and texts both sides. No phone tag, no sticky notes.

What happens when a call is urgent?

You set the triage rules; AVA runs them. A burst pipe, sparks at an outlet, no heat in January — whatever you define as urgent gets flagged on the call, moved to the front of the schedule or transferred live, and your cell gets an alert immediately. Routine calls book like routine calls. Emergencies act like emergencies.

Can AVA work with my existing phone number?

Yes. Nothing changes for your customers — they dial the number they've always dialed. We launch AVA on your existing line, and your forwarding rules decide what it takes: every call, after-hours only, or just the overflow when your team is already on the phone.

Does AVA replace my receptionist or dispatcher?

No. AVA covers what they physically can't: 2 AM, Sunday, both lines ringing at once, and the calls that come in while they're already helping someone. Keep your people on the work only people can do — AVA takes the ringing phone they had to keep dropping everything for.

What happens when AVA cannot handle a call?

It follows the fallback rule you chose: transfer straight to your cell, take a message and text it to you, or schedule a callback. You define the trigger words, the VIP callers, and the situations that always go to a human. You can also take over or pause AVA any time you want.

How much does AVA cost?

The Starter System is $497 a month plus a $497 one-time setup. Setup is the done-for-you part: we map your calls, build AVA around them, test it with you on real calls, launch it on your number, and keep running and tuning it after it's live. Growth is $997 a month plus a $1,500 setup. Operations builds start around $1,997 a month and are scoped on your call volume. Voice minutes bill by usage. No contracts — cancel anytime.

“Is it worth $497 a month?”

Run it against your own numbers, not ours. Take your average job value and count the calls that hit voicemail last month. If AVA books even one job a month you would have missed, check the math for your trade — then run your own numbers in the calculator.
