// 03.5 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions operators ask.

[01]

What does AVA do?

AVA answers your inbound calls, captures trip details in real conversation, qualifies the request, and routes everything to your team — your dispatch software, CRM, booking form, or shared inbox. Your dispatcher confirms the trip instead of starting from scratch.

[02]

Does AVA replace my dispatcher?

No. AVA picks up when your team can't — peak hours, after-hours, three calls at once. Your dispatcher still runs the operation. AVA just makes sure the next ride doesn't go to whoever answers first.

[03]

Can AVA handle credit card payments?

AVA captures everything except the card. After the call, AVA sends a secure payment link by SMS using your existing processor — Stripe, Square, Limo Anywhere Pay, whatever you already use. AVA never touches the money. You keep your processor, your margins, your compliance.

[04]

What software does AVA work with?

AVA works alongside your dispatch software, CRM, booking form, or shared inbox. Whether you run Limo Anywhere, FastTrack, Livery Coach, a lighter setup, or just a phone and a notepad — AVA fits around how your team already takes and confirms trips.

[05]

How fast can I get this running?

Starter is 1 line, scoped to a 1-3 vehicle operation, typically running within a week of scoping. Pro and Enterprise builds take longer because the routing and handoff rules are more complex. Every setup gets a 20-minute scoping call first.
