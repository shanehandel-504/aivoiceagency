Questions operators ask.

[01]

What does AVA do?

AVA answers your business phone twenty-four seven, captures every caller's request in real conversation, qualifies what they need, and routes the details to your team. No more missed calls. No more voicemail leakage.

[02]

How fast does AVA pick up?

AVA answers when your team can't. No voicemail maze. No dead air. Call (786) 937-1218 and hear AVA live.

[03]

Does AVA replace my staff?

No. AVA handles the intake load so your team handles the work that actually grows the business. Three calls hit at once — AVA gets all three. Your team works the trips, the appointments, the jobs.

[04]

Can AVA send calls into the system I already use?

Yes. AVA pushes captured details into your CRM, scheduling software, booking form, or shared inbox — whatever you already have.

Two to three hours for Starter. No hardware. No software install. Call forwarding from your existing number. Every setup gets a twenty-minute scoping call first.

What industries does AVA work for?

Any business that takes inbound calls — professional services, home services, medical practices, ground transportation, hospitality. Each AVA is customized to your business.
