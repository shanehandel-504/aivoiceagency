// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions from Milwaukee plumbing shops.

[01]

What does AVA actually capture on a burst-pipe call?

Address first, then the failure point if the caller knows it, whether the water is shut off, property access details, and callback number. The whole package routes to your on-call plumber by SMS the moment the call ends.

[02]

Can AVA walk a panicking caller to the main shutoff?

AVA keeps the caller focused and captures the situation, and it can relay your standard guidance — like locating the main shutoff — exactly as you script it. Anything beyond your script gets routed to a human immediately.

[03]

We run Jobber. Does that work?

Yes. Captured emergencies, drain calls, and estimate requests land in the scheduling and CRM stack you already use, tagged so your dispatcher can see urgency at a glance.

[04]

Do you handle the North Shore and the south suburbs?

AVA covers your map, not ours — Shorewood, Whitefish Bay, Riverwest, Walker's Point, Greenfield, Cudahy, Franklin, wherever you run trucks. Out-of-area callers get politely screened out, so your dispatcher stays focused on the jobs you actually run.

[05]

What about gas-smell calls?

Safety scripts are non-negotiable: a caller reporting a gas odor is told to leave the building and call We Energies and 911 first. AVA captures the details for your follow-up but never schedules over a safety hazard.
