// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions operators ask.

[01]

Can AVA dispatch emergency calls?

AVA captures emergency details — no heat, no water, electrical hazard — and routes the call to the on-call tech by SMS, phone, or your dispatch software. Built to capture the call before it goes cold — no voicemail at 11pm.

[02]

Does AVA work with ServiceTitan, Jobber, Housecall Pro, or FieldEdge?

Yes. AVA pushes captured jobs, estimates, and schedule changes into whatever scheduling and CRM tool you already run.

[03]

Can AVA tell a repeat customer from a new one?

Yes. AVA recognizes returning callers, pulls their job history, and routes them differently than a quote-shopper hitting your number for the first time.

[04]

What about peak season — storm calls, heat waves, freezes?

AVA answers every call. Two hundred plus simultaneous calls. Your team works the jobs that pay; AVA handles the intake load.

[05]

Will AVA quote prices?

AVA captures the job scope and books the estimate or service call. Pricing decisions stay with your team — AVA never freelances on quotes.
