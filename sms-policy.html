SMS & A2P Messaging

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This page explains the SMS messaging program operated by AI Voice Agency ("we," "us," "AVA") under A2P 10DLC compliance. It covers what messages we send, how you consent to receive them, and how to opt out at any time.

AI Voice Agency uses 350-220-5305 for setup confirmation and SMS follow-up. By submitting a contact form, you consent to receive SMS messages about your AVA scoping call and setup. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

1. Program Description

Our SMS program is a transactional and conversational messaging program used to:

  • Confirm scheduled scoping and onboarding calls
  • Send setup, deployment, and configuration status updates
  • Reply to operator support questions during active setup
  • Follow up after a scoping call with a written summary or next-step link

We do not send marketing blasts or promotional content on this number.

2. Consent & Opt-In

You consent to receive SMS messages from 350-220-5305 when you do any of the following:

  • Submit a contact form on aivoiceagency.ai that includes your phone number
  • Book a scoping call through our calendar widget
  • Reply to an inbound message we send confirming your interest

Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message frequency varies based on your active engagement with us — typically fewer than 10 messages per week during an active setup, and fewer than 2 per month otherwise.

3. Opt-Out (STOP)

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time. Reply STOP to any message we send from 350-220-5305 and you will be removed from the program. You will receive a single confirmation message and then no further SMS from us.

If you later want to re-subscribe, reply START or contact us directly.

4. Help (HELP)

Reply HELP to any message and you'll receive contact information for our team plus a link back to this page.

5. Message & Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. We do not charge any additional fee for receiving messages from us.

6. Supported Carriers

Our messaging program supports all major US carriers including AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, Sprint, US Cellular, and others. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

7. Privacy

Phone numbers and SMS content are handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy. We do not share your number with third parties for their own marketing.
