// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions Wisconsin operators ask.

[01]

Does AVA integrate with LimoAnywhere, FASTTRAK, or Livery Coach?

Yes. AVA pushes captured trips into your dispatch software in real time — Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Livery Coach, RideXpert, or whatever you run.

[02]

Can AVA handle The Crush — Friday 4:47 PM rush?

AVA answers two hundred plus simultaneous calls. No wait times. No voicemail leakage during peak. The 4:47 corporate-confirm wave gets caught — operator-grade intake on every call.

[03]

How does AVA handle overnight TMC and corporate concierge calls?

AVA never sleeps. Two AM Marriott airport pickup gets the same operator-grade intake as two PM. TMC partners and corporate concierges get the same intake they expect from a fifty-person dispatch room.

[04]

Does AVA know Wisconsin vehicle types and pickup locations?

Sedan, SUV, Stretch, Sprinter, Mini-coach, Motorcoach, Party bus, Executive van — all locked. Waukesha, Kewaskum, Oconomowoc, Sheboygan, Ashwaubenon, Wauwatosa, Lambeau — all pronounced correctly.

[05]

Can AVA route affiliate overflow through GNet?

Yes. When capacity hits ceiling, AVA can farm overflow via GNet to your affiliate network — keeping the relationship and the commission instead of losing the trip entirely.

[06]

Will AVA replace my dispatcher?

No. AVA replaces the intake load — your dispatcher dispatches. AVA captures the trip; your dispatcher confirms the driver, runs the manifest, and keeps the operation moving.
