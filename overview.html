What AVA is

A receptionist that never misses the phone.

Not a chatbot and not a call center — a voice agent that runs your intake by your rules, on the number you already use.

Answers in one ring, any hour

Nights, weekends, and the overflow when both lines are ringing at once. Callers get a real answer instead of voicemail — AVA doesn't pretend to be a person and gets straight to what's wrong, how urgent it is, and when someone can come.

Books the job onto your calendar

AVA offers real open slots, holds the appointment, writes the job into your CRM if you use one, and texts both sides a confirmation. No phone tag, no sticky notes.

Recovers the calls you'd miss

Missed calls get a text back in seconds so the lead doesn't move on to the next name in the search results. Every call is summarized, recorded, and transcribed for you.

Runs on your playbook

You set the triage rules, the emergency triggers, the VIP callers, and where each kind of call should go. When AVA can't handle something, it follows the fallback you chose — transfer, message, or callback.

How the build works

Done for you — you approve it before it goes live.

  • 1 · We map your calls. We start with call mapping: your services, your service area, your booking rules, what counts as an emergency, and where each kind of call should go.
  • 2 · We build AVA around them. AVA answers from your playbook, not a generic script. We connect it to the calendar you already run your day from and any CRM you use.
  • 3 · You test and approve. You hear AVA on real test calls and sign off with your own ears. It never takes a live call until you say go.
  • 4 · We launch and keep tuning. We launch AVA on your existing number, then keep running and tuning it after it's live. Your forwarding rules decide what it takes — every call, after-hours only, or just the overflow.
