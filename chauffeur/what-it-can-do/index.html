FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Can it transfer to a person?
Yes. When a caller asks for a person, it puts the caller through to your team: your owner, your on-call person or your dispatcher.
Does it know repeat customers?
Yes, with your CRM. It remembers your regular customers by their phone number and greets them by name.
Can it work with the software I already use?
Yes. It works with Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Moovs, Livery Coach, GNet, Hudson, SantaCruz (GroundWidgets), LimoExpress, A to Z Dispatch and RideBits. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into your software.
Does it speak other languages?
Yes, as an add-on. More than 50 languages, priced on a quote.
