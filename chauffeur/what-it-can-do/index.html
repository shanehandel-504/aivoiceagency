FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Can it transfer to a person?
Yes, once it is set up for your company. It hands the call to your owner, your on-call person, or your dispatcher — any time, or only when the caller needs a person.
Does it know repeat customers?
It can. It is set up to recognize repeat callers by their phone number and greet them by name.
Can it work with the software I already use?
It can be connected to the software you already run your trips in. Then the trip lands where your dispatcher works.
Does it speak Spanish?
It can take calls in Spanish. It is turned on when we build your desk.
