FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Do I have to change dispatch software?
No. Nothing is replaced and no records move. AI Chauffeur answers your phone, in front of everything else you run. It works with Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Moovs, Livery Coach, GNet, Hudson, SantaCruz (GroundWidgets), LimoExpress, A to Z Dispatch and RideBits, or just a shared inbox. Keep what you run, and keep running it the same way.
Does it put the trip into my system for me?
Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. With your CRM, the trip is also written straight into your software, and we connect it at setup. Either way, your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur.
What if I just use a shared inbox?
That works. Plenty of operators run the phone with an inbox and a notepad. The trip sheet reads the same either way: pickup, dropoff, time, vehicle class, passenger and bag count, flight number and terminal, and notes. Your dispatcher reads it and assigns the car and the chauffeur.
How does AI Chauffeur work with Limo Anywhere?
It answers the call, takes the whole trip and books it. Right after the call, the person on dispatch has the trip sheet. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into Limo Anywhere, connected at setup. The field-by-field answer is on the Limo Anywhere page.
How does AI Chauffeur work with FASTTRAK?
AI Chauffeur takes the trip on the phone and books it. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into FASTTRAK, connected at setup. We go over the details on the setup call. The corporate-fleet answer is on the FASTTRAK page.
How does AI Chauffeur work with SantaCruz (GroundWidgets)?
There is nothing to install on the SantaCruz side. AI Chauffeur books the trip on the call and sends the trip sheet to your dispatcher. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into SantaCruz, connected at setup. Which fields the trip sheet carries is set on the setup call.
What if we just run a shared inbox?
That is a system, and it is the simplest one to set up. The trip sheet, the recording and the transcript arrive in the inbox your team already watches. Whoever is on dispatch takes it from there. The address and the subject line are set on the setup call.
How is the trip sheet set up?
On the setup call. Our team sets it up for your company. The call covers what you already run, who gets the trip sheet, and how it reaches that person. Then we load your rates, your service area and your fleet, build it, make test calls, and go live. Typically one to two weeks.
