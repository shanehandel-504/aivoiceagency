FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Is this just basic call coverage?
No. AI Chauffeur is dispatch intake software for limo and black car operators. AVA captures ride requests, organizes trip details, estimates the quote range, and hands your dispatcher a finished trip ticket. Call coverage is a side effect, not the product.
Does it replace my dispatcher?
No. It handles the first layer of intake so your dispatcher is not buried by missed calls, repeated questions, and after-hours requests. Your dispatcher confirms and runs the operation.
What still requires dispatcher approval?
Every trip does. AVA captures the request and builds the ticket; a human confirms it, assigns the vehicle, and books it in your dispatch software. AVA does not write into Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, or Santa Cruz, does not take payment, and does not commit a vehicle on its own.
How does AI Chauffeur handle multiple calls at once?
AVA answers every inbound line at the same time, so no caller waits on hold. Each call gets its own intake: pickup, dropoff, time, vehicle class, passengers, bags, and flight details. Every one ends as a separate trip ticket sitting in front of your dispatcher, ready to confirm.
Can it quote rides?
Yes, when your rate rules are configured. The page demo uses illustrative quote ranges only. Live setup requires your real rate rules, service area, vehicle types, and dispatch process.
Does it work with my CRM or dispatch software?
Yes. The call comes back as a clean trip ticket your dispatcher confirms in the software you already run — Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Santa Cruz, or a shared inbox. Nothing gets ripped out and nothing gets replaced.
How fast can it go live?
Setup is done for you by our team. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once your rate rules, service area, and dispatch process are documented.
What about pricing?
Every build is custom to your operation — your rates, your service area, your vehicle classes. We scope it on the setup call, once we know your call volume, fleet size, and how you dispatch. That call runs 20 minutes.
Can I just hear it first?
Call (414) 775-0019 and AVA picks up. That is the same line an operator's caller would reach — no booking, no form, no salesperson. Listen to how it handles you, then decide.
