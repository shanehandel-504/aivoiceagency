How it works

Four stages. One clean record.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Is this just basic call coverage?
No. AIChauffeur is dispatch intake software powered by AI voice. It captures ride requests, organizes trip details, estimates the quote range, and routes the request into your workflow. Call coverage is a side effect, not the product.
Does it replace my dispatcher?
No. It handles the first layer of intake so your dispatcher is not buried by missed calls, repeated questions, after-hours requests, and basic confirmation work. Your dispatcher confirms and runs the operation.
Can it quote rides?
Yes, when your rate rules are configured. The page demo uses illustrative quote ranges only. Live setup requires your real rate rules, service area, vehicle types, and dispatch process.
Does it work with my CRM or dispatch software?
Setup is custom. We connect AIChauffeur to your existing dispatch workflow, CRM, or spreadsheet so trip requests land where your team already works.
How fast can it go live?
Local setup is founder-led right now. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once your rate rules, service area, and dispatch process are documented.
What about pricing?
Pricing is set during the local setup call once we understand your call volume, fleet size, and dispatch process. The intro call is 20 minutes.
