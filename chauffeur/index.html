FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Is this just basic call coverage?
No. AI Chauffeur answers your phones and books the trips. It takes the whole trip, quotes your rates, and puts it in your CRM.
Does it replace my dispatcher?
No. It takes the phones so your dispatcher is not buried by missed calls and repeated questions. Your people run the cars, and your dispatcher still runs the operation. Which half of the job gets automated, and which half never will, is set out on limo dispatch automation.
What still requires dispatcher approval?
Assigning the car and the driver. AI Chauffeur books the trip on the call and puts it in your CRM, and anything missing is flagged for review, so nothing slips.
How does AI Chauffeur handle multiple calls at once?
It answers every line at the same time, one call or a thousand, so no caller waits on hold. Each call gets its own trip: pickup, drop-off, time, vehicle, passengers, bags, and flight. Every one lands as its own trip sheet.
Can it quote rides?
Yes. It quotes your rates on the call and gives one clean number, service fee included. The demo runs a sample trip ticket. Yours runs your rates.
Does it work with my CRM or dispatch software?
Yes. It puts the trip in your CRM, and it can be connected to the dispatch software you already run. Where each platform stands is on the integrations page.
How fast can it go live?
Setup is done for you by our team. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once your rate rules, service area, and dispatch process are documented.
What about pricing?
Every build is custom to your operation — your rates, your service area, your vehicle classes. We scope it on the setup call, once we know your call volume, fleet size, and how you dispatch. That call runs 20 minutes.
Can I just hear it first?
Yes. Call (414) 775-0019 and AI Chauffeur picks up. It is the same agent your callers would reach, with no form and no salesperson.
