FAQ

Questions from operators.

Is this just basic call coverage?
No. AI Chauffeur answers your phones and books the trips. It takes the whole trip, quotes your rates, and sends the trip sheet to your dispatcher.
Does it replace my dispatcher?
No. It answers the calls you send it, takes the reservation and sends the trip sheet to your dispatcher. Your dispatcher confirms the car and the chauffeur.
What still requires dispatcher approval?
Assigning the car and the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur books the trip on the call and sends the trip sheet to your dispatcher. Anything missing is flagged for dispatch, so nothing slips.
How does AI Chauffeur handle multiple calls at once?
It answers every call at the same time, so no caller waits on hold. Each call gets its own trip sheet.
Can it quote rides?
Yes. It quotes from your rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day, in your service area, and gives one clean number. A trip your rates don't cover goes to your dispatcher to quote. The sample calls on this page use sample rates. Yours runs your rates.
Does it work with my reservation software or dispatch software?
Yes. It works with Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Moovs, Livery Coach, GNet, Hudson, SantaCruz (GroundWidgets), LimoExpress, A to Z Dispatch and RideBits. With your reservation software (the $1,697 plan), the trip is written straight into your software. Where each platform stands is on the integrations page.
How fast can it go live?
Our team sets it up for you. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once we have your rates, your service area and who gets the trip sheet.
