Questions

Is there a contract?
No. Month to month.
What does the setup fee cover?
Building it for your company: your name, your service area, your fleet, your rates and who gets the trip sheet.
Does it quote my rates?
Yes. Set up for your company, it quotes from your rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day, in your service area. A trip your rates don't cover goes to your dispatcher to quote.
Does it work with my reservation software?
Yes. It works with Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Moovs, Livery Coach, GNet, Hudson, SantaCruz (GroundWidgets), LimoExpress, A to Z Dispatch and RideBits. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into your software.
How much is AI Chauffeur with my CRM?
$1,697 a month plus a $1,697 one-time setup. It writes the trip straight into your reservation software, remembers your regular customers, and sends a card link when there's no card on file.
Do I pay for talk time?
Yes. Talk time is 65 cents a minute from the first minute, on every plan. No plan comes with free minutes.
Does the price go up for a bigger fleet?
No. The monthly price is the same for 3 cars or 300. A bigger fleet just uses more talk time.
What if you can't connect my software?
If we can't connect your software within 30 days, you get a full refund within 10 days.
Does it track flights?
Yes, as an add-on. It takes the flight number on the call, and flight tracking watches the flight for your dispatcher. Priced on a quote.
What add-ons can I get?
More than 50 languages, flight tracking, reminder texts, and a talk button for your website. Each one is priced on a quote.
Can it answer only after hours, or only the calls we miss?
Yes. 24/7, only after hours, or only the calls your office misses. You pick.
Is this a cheap bot with our name on it?
No. We build it for your company: your fleet, your rates, your service area, your name on the line. It's not a $99 bot with your name typed in.
What does my dispatcher get after a call?
Right after the call: the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur.
