About GNet

What GNet is known for.

Known for
GNet is GRiDD's network for sending trips between operators. GNet says it is not a booking system. It links the dispatch software you run to your affiliates.
Built for
GNet is for chauffeur and black car operators who farm trips in and out. GNet says it works across more than 65 dispatch systems, Limo Anywhere and FASTTRAK among them.
How it connects
GNet publishes an API, with webhooks that report changes on trips you send. AI Chauffeur connects to GNet at setup through its API, webhook or email, scoped on your setup call.

So GNet is not where your own bookings live. Your reservation software is. GNet is how a trip moves to an affiliate when your team decides to farm it out. AI Chauffeur does not farm trips out. Your dispatcher makes that call.

Day one

What GNet operators get on day one.

From the first day the line is live, a GNet operator gets a written-up call every time. Nothing has to be connected.

  • Calls answered in your company's name, so the caller never hears a stranger's name.
  • A price from your own rate sheet, in your service area.
  • The trip sheet by email and text to whoever is on dispatch, once the call ends.
  • The recording and the transcript, with each trip sheet.
  • A live transfer to your team when a caller asks for someone.
  • Your client dashboard is included with both plans. Every trip sheet also comes with the call recording.

Booked solid that night? The trip sheet is still written. Your team can read it early and decide to run the trip or send it out through GNet.

The $1,697 plan

What the $1,697 plan adds for a GNet operator.

With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation is written into your reservation software, the one GNet links to your affiliates. From there your team works it like any other trip.

  • The new booking entered in your reservation software, not typed from a note.
  • Regular customers remembered from one call to the next.
  • A card link when the caller has no card on file.

Changes and cancellations go to your dispatcher. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch. It never moves a trip in GNet, and it never sends a trip to an affiliate on its own.

AI Chauffeur connects to GNet at setup through its API, webhook or email, scoped on your setup call. If we can't connect your software within 30 days, you get a full refund within 10 days.

FAQ

Questions from GNet operators.

Does AI Chauffeur connect to GNet?
Yes. AI Chauffeur connects to GNet at setup through its API, webhook or email, scoped on your setup call. Your new bookings go into your reservation software. Your farm-outs keep moving through GNet the way they do now.
What goes into GNet and what goes to my dispatcher?
AI Chauffeur does not send trips out through GNet on its own. With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation goes into your reservation software. If your dispatcher farms it out, it moves through GNet from there. Your dispatcher gets the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript on both plans.
Does it change or cancel trips in GNet?
No. It takes the details and sends them to your dispatcher. Your team decides what changes, in GNet or anywhere else.
