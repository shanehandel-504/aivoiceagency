About SantaCruz (GroundWidgets)

What SantaCruz is known for.

Known for
SantaCruz is the core of GroundWidgets. It covers reservations, dispatch and accounting for ground transportation companies.
Built for
GroundWidgets writes for established operators. That means companies with one location and companies that serve many markets.
How it connects
GroundWidgets' WebConnect booking tool sends emailed confirmations. AI Chauffeur connects to SantaCruz through email dispatch or webhook, scoped on your setup call.

Your reservations, accounts and history stay in SantaCruz. AI Chauffeur adds one thing in front of it: a phone that gets picked up, written up and booked.

Day one

What SantaCruz (GroundWidgets) operators get on day one.

As soon as your line is live, each call comes with a full write-up. Nothing in SantaCruz has to be touched first.

  • Calls answered in your company's name, around the clock if you want.
  • Prices from your own rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day.
  • A trip sheet to your dispatcher by email and text, the minute the call ends.
  • The recording and the transcript for every call.
  • A live transfer to your team for a caller who asks for a person.
  • Your client dashboard is included with both plans. Every trip sheet also comes with the call recording.

Your dispatcher types the trip into SantaCruz from the trip sheet. Each detail is in order, so it is quick.

The $1,697 plan writes the new reservation straight into SantaCruz. Your team sees it there with the rest of the day's work.

FAQ

Questions from SantaCruz operators.

Does AI Chauffeur connect to SantaCruz?
Yes. AI Chauffeur connects to SantaCruz through email dispatch or webhook, scoped on your setup call. Before it is connected, you still get the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript on every call.
What goes into SantaCruz and what goes to my dispatcher?
With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation goes into SantaCruz: pickup, drop-off, time, flight, vehicle, riders, bags and notes. Your dispatcher gets the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript on both plans.
Does it change or cancel trips in SantaCruz?
No. It takes the details and sends them to your dispatcher. Any change in SantaCruz is made by your team.
