About LimoExpress

What LimoExpress is known for.

Known for
LimoExpress is software for limo and livery companies. It runs a trip from the first question to the payment.
Built for
LimoExpress says it fits companies of all sizes. Its plans go from 5 to 50 user accounts, with no limit on vehicles.
How it connects
LimoExpress publishes an API guide, and its plans list an API. It can also send email and text notices to your clients. AI Chauffeur connects to LimoExpress at setup through its API, webhook or email, scoped on your setup call.

LimoExpress also offers welcome signs and flight tracking for airport transfers. AI Chauffeur fits in front of that: it takes the airport call and writes it up.

Day one

What LimoExpress operators get on day one.

The day your line goes live, every call comes with a write-up. You can connect LimoExpress later.

  • Every call picked up in your company's name.
  • A quote from your own rate sheet, in your service area.
  • A trip sheet for your dispatcher, by email and by text, as the call ends.
  • The recording and the transcript, attached to each trip sheet.
  • A caller who wants a person is put through to your team.
  • Your client dashboard is included with both plans. Every trip sheet also comes with the call recording.

Your dispatcher enters the trip in LimoExpress from the trip sheet. The flight, the sign name and the bags are already written down.

The $1,697 plan

What the $1,697 plan adds in LimoExpress.

With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation goes straight into LimoExpress. It is waiting there when your team looks.

  • The new booking entered in LimoExpress, with no retyping.
  • Your regular customers remembered by name.
  • A card link sent to a caller who has no card on file.

Changes and cancellations go to your dispatcher. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch. It never moves or cancels a trip in LimoExpress. Your team assigns the car and the chauffeur.

FAQ

Questions from LimoExpress operators.

Does AI Chauffeur connect to LimoExpress?
Yes. AI Chauffeur connects to LimoExpress at setup through its API, webhook or email, scoped on your setup call. On day one you already get the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. The $1,697 plan adds the booking in LimoExpress.
What goes into LimoExpress and what goes to my dispatcher?
With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation goes into LimoExpress: pickup, drop-off, time, flight, vehicle, passengers, bags and notes. On both plans, your dispatcher gets the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript.
Does it change or cancel trips in LimoExpress?
No. It takes the details and sends them to your dispatcher. Your dispatcher makes the change in LimoExpress.
