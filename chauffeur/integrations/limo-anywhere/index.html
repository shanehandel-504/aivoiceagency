AI Chauffeur works beside Limo Anywhere.

Yes. AI Chauffeur works beside Limo Anywhere. It answers your line, takes the whole trip and books it. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. With your reservation software, the trip is written straight into Limo Anywhere. Connected at setup. Nothing is ripped out and nothing is moved.

Last updated 20 October 2026

What With your reservation software means here

A connection set up for your company.

With your reservation software, AI Chauffeur writes the trip straight into Limo Anywhere. We connect it at setup. We match it to the way you already work: your service types, your vehicle classes, your rates, your required fields and your statuses.

That matching is the work. A connection that ignores your service types will file a wedding as an airport run. One that ignores your required fields makes a record your dispatcher has to reopen anyway. One that ignores your statuses puts a trip where your team won't look for it. Matching those is what we do at setup.

On the base plan, nothing is written into Limo Anywhere. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript, and enters the trip the way they do now. The integrations page shows where every other platform stands.

Day one, and with your reservation software

Two ways to run it, and both start on day one.

Nobody waits on a connection to start booking calls. The phone is worth answering on day one, whether or not your software is connected yet.

Day one
The trip sheet comes to your inbox. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Nothing touches Limo Anywhere. Your dispatcher enters the reservation the way they always have, with every detail already taken down instead of a voicemail to replay.
At setup
We connect Limo Anywhere. Your service types, vehicle classes, rates, required fields and statuses are matched to your company. Then we run test trips until the records look the way your dispatcher expects. We go over it on the setup call.
With your reservation software
It's written into your software. The trip goes straight into Limo Anywhere. AI Chauffeur also remembers your regular customers, and sends a card link when there's no card on file. Your dispatcher still assigns the car and the chauffeur.
Staying on day one
That's a fine way to run it. If you'd rather a person enters every trip, stay on the base plan and nothing gets connected. The answering, the quoting and the booking all work the same.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Do I have to migrate anything out of Limo Anywhere?
No. There is no export, no import, and no records to move. Your reservations, rates, accounts and history all stay exactly where they are. AI Chauffeur arrives in front of the operation, on the phone line, and the first thing it changes is how many calls get answered — not where anything is stored.
With your reservation software, what does AI Chauffeur write into Limo Anywhere?
The trip, as a reservation: pickup, dropoff, date and time, vehicle, passengers, bags, flight, callback number and notes. We connect it at setup and match it to your service types, vehicle classes and statuses. That way it looks like the records your dispatcher already works with, not a strange entry in the list.
Can I stay on the base plan and never connect Limo Anywhere?
Yes. The trip sheet comes with every plan, and it is a complete way to run this. Stay on the base plan and nothing gets connected. You can also start there and move to With your reservation software later.
Could a car go out without anyone looking at the trip?
No. AI Chauffeur books the trip on the call. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur, on either plan. AI Chauffeur does not assign a vehicle and does not take payment.
