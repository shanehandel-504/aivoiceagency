Integration · Limo Anywhere

AVA works beside Limo Anywhere.

Yes — AVA works beside Limo Anywhere. It answers your line, takes the whole trip, and delivers a complete ticket to the person on dispatch, who confirms it in Limo Anywhere. Nothing is ripped out and nothing is migrated. Direct write-back is configured and verified against your own account before it is switched on.

Last updated 7 August 2026

What write-back means here

A connection built around your account.

AVA can create quote requests and reservation records in supported dispatch systems. Every connection is configured and tested around your account — service types, vehicle classes, rate rules, required fields, statuses, and approval workflow — before direct write-back is activated.

Read the middle of that sentence twice, because it is the part that matters. A connection that ignores your service types will file a wedding as an airport run. One that ignores your required fields will create a record your dispatcher has to reopen anyway. One that ignores your approval workflow will put a trip somewhere it has not been approved to be. Configuring those is the work, and it is why write-back is switched on after setup verification rather than on the first day.

Until it is switched on, delivery is a completed trip ticket to the person on dispatch — which is a whole product on its own, and the one most operators start with. The integration status table says where every other platform stands.

The connection ladder

Three rungs, and you can stop on any of them.

Nobody waits on integration work to start capturing calls. The rungs run in this order because the phone is worth answering on day one whether or not anything is connected yet.

Rung 1 · Day one
Shared inbox delivery. The completed ticket lands in the inbox or channel your team already watches. Nothing touches Limo Anywhere. Your dispatcher reads the ticket and enters the reservation the way they always have — with every detail already taken down, instead of a voicemail to replay.
Rung 2 · During setup
The connection is configured and tested. Your service types, vehicle classes, rate rules, required fields, statuses and approval workflow get matched to your account, then run against test trips until the records come out the shape your dispatcher expects. This is scoped on the setup call.
Rung 3 · After verification
Direct write-back is activated. Quote requests and reservation records are created for you. Your dispatcher still confirms every trip inside the approval workflow you already run — write-back changes where the record is created, not who says yes to it.
Staying on rung one
A legitimate answer, and a common one. If you would rather every record was keyed by a person who read the ticket first, say so on the setup call and nothing gets connected. The answering, the capture and the quoting all work the same.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Do I have to migrate anything out of Limo Anywhere?
No. There is no export, no import, and no records to move. Your reservations, rates, accounts and history all stay exactly where they are. AVA arrives in front of the operation, on the phone line, and the first thing it changes is how many calls get answered — not where anything is stored.
What does AVA create in Limo Anywhere once write-back is on?
Quote requests and reservation records, on supported dispatch systems, once the connection has been configured and tested against your account. What a new record contains and which status it opens in are part of that configuration, so it comes out looking like the records your dispatcher already works with rather than a foreign object in the list.
Can I keep AVA on shared-inbox delivery and never turn write-back on?
Yes, and plenty of operators do. Ticket delivery is included in every setup and it is a complete way to run this. Say so on the setup call and no connection gets built. You can also start there and move later, because the ladder runs in that order for exactly this reason.
Could a trip go live without anyone looking at it?
No. Your dispatcher confirms every trip, on every rung of the ladder. Your approval workflow is one of the things configured and tested before write-back is switched on, so an automatically created record still has to pass through the same approval your operation uses today. AVA does not commit a vehicle and does not take payment.
