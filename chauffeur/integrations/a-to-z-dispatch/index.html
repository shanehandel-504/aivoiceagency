About A to Z Dispatch

What A to Z Dispatch is known for.

Known for
A to Z Dispatch is white-label dispatch software for limo, taxi, chauffeur and airport transfer companies. It takes no commission on fares.
Built for
A to Z Dispatch says it is built for small and mid-sized operators with 1 to 100 vehicles.
How it connects
A to Z Dispatch lists email confirmations, plus text and email notices as an add-on. AI Chauffeur connects to A to Z Dispatch through email dispatch or webhook, scoped on your setup call.

A small fleet often means the owner is also the dispatcher, and sometimes the driver. AI Chauffeur picks up when you can't, and writes the call up so you can act on it later.

Day one

What A to Z Dispatch operators get on day one.

From the day your line goes live, every call comes with a full write-up. You do not have to connect anything first.

  • Your calls answered in your company's name, even while you drive.
  • A price from your own rate sheet.
  • A trip sheet to you or your dispatcher by email and text, right after the call.
  • The call recording and the transcript.
  • A live transfer to your team when a caller asks for a person.
  • Your client dashboard is included with both plans. Every trip sheet also comes with the call recording.

You read the trip sheet and enter the trip in A to Z Dispatch. Child seats, bags and the flight are already written down.

The $1,697 plan

What the $1,697 plan adds in A to Z Dispatch.

The $1,697 plan writes the new reservation straight into A to Z Dispatch. When you open it, the trip is there.

  • New bookings entered in A to Z Dispatch for you.

FAQ

Questions from A to Z Dispatch operators.

Does AI Chauffeur connect to A to Z Dispatch?
Yes. AI Chauffeur connects to A to Z Dispatch through email dispatch or webhook, scoped on your setup call. Until then, every call still brings the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript.
What goes into A to Z Dispatch and what goes to my dispatcher?
With the $1,697 plan, the new reservation goes into A to Z Dispatch: pickup, drop-off, time, flight, vehicle, riders, bags and notes. On both plans, the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript go to you or your dispatcher.
Does it change or cancel trips in A to Z Dispatch?
No. It takes the details and sends them to your dispatcher. You make the change in A to Z Dispatch.
What if you can't connect it?
If we can't connect your software within 30 days, you get a full refund within 10 days.
How much is it?
Reservation Desk is $997 a month plus a $997 one-time setup. It quotes your rates and books the reservation. With Your Reservation Software is $1,697 a month plus a $1,697 one-time setup. It quotes your rates, books the reservation and also writes the booking into A to Z Dispatch.
