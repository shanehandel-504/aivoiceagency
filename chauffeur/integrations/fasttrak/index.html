Where this one stands

FASTTRAK: Connected at setup.

That's the same status shown on the integrations page. With your reservation software, AI Chauffeur writes the trip straight into FASTTRAK. We go over your FASTTRAK setup on the setup call: your service types, vehicle classes, rates and required fields.

The base plan works from day one. AI Chauffeur answers your dispatch line and books the trip. Right after the call, whoever is on dispatch has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript, in the inbox your team already watches. That comes with every plan and needs nothing at the FASTTRAK end.

Nobody's line waits on a connection. The trip sheet comes first. With your reservation software, the trip is also written into FASTTRAK.

The operation this fits

More cars means more calls that are not simple.

A two-car operator gets one kind of call. A fleet running corporate accounts gets a different one, and it is rarely a passenger booking their own ride. It is an assistant three time zones away, a travel desk with a cost centre to quote against, or a road-show coordinator with six stops and a flight at the end of it.

Those calls cost the most to fumble, and a rushed call fumbles them first. The booker's name gets taken and the passenger's does not. The account never makes it onto the trip sheet. Then dispatch spends the afternoon calling back to ask.

Corporate accounts

AI Chauffeur takes the account or company name as spoken and puts it at the top of the trip sheet, so dispatch can match it to the corporate profile you already keep instead of guessing from a phone number.

Bookers who never ride

Caller and passenger are two different people on most corporate calls. AI Chauffeur takes both, and takes a callback number for the person who actually needs to be reached when a flight moves.

Multi-stop days

A road show is one booking with a list in it. The stops come down in order, with the timing the caller gave for each, rather than collapsing into a pickup and a dropoff.

Farm-in and farm-out

AI Chauffeur does not make that call and does not send work anywhere. It puts the complete trip in front of your team early enough that they can make the decision while there is still time to make it.

Every call at once

Three callers at 4 PM on a Thursday get three trip sheets at 4 PM on a Thursday. No hold queue, no call-back queue, and nothing deciding which caller mattered more.

Your rates

Quotes come from your rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day, in your service area. A trip your rates don't cover goes to your dispatcher to quote. AI Chauffeur never invents a number.

Corporate calls

What AI Chauffeur asks for, and what it does with the answer.

The corporate call has more moving parts than a night out, and this is the whole list. Anything AI Chauffeur is not certain of gets read back before the call ends.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

We run a bigger fleet with account managers. Does AI Chauffeur fit that?
It fits the phone side of it. AI Chauffeur answers every call and takes corporate calls properly: account, booker, passenger, stops, flight and callback. Right after the call, whoever is on dispatch has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Account management, pricing decisions and the relationship stay with the people who own them. AI Chauffeur doesn't touch any of that.
What do we go over on the FASTTRAK setup call?
What your FASTTRAK account supports and what your fleet needs. Then your service types, vehicle classes, rates, required fields, and the way your dispatchers already work. With your reservation software, that's how the trip gets written straight into FASTTRAK. Twenty minutes, and it ends with a plan.
Does AI Chauffeur farm work in or out on its own?
No. It does not send work anywhere, accept work from anywhere, or decide that a trip is outside your area. When the caller says something that bears on that, the trip sheet says it plainly and early, while your team still has time to place the trip.
How does AI Chauffeur handle a corporate booker who is not the passenger?
As two people, because they are two people. AI Chauffeur takes the booker's name and callback number, then the passenger's name separately, and puts both on the trip sheet. That is the single most common way a rushed corporate call goes wrong, and it is why the question gets asked on the call rather than guessed at afterwards.
