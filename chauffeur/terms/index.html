2. What the service does

AI Chauffeur builds and runs AVA, an AI voice agent, on your inbound line. AVA answers calls, asks the questions a dispatcher would ask, and captures the trip: pickup, dropoff, time, vehicle class, passenger and bag count, flight details and notes. The call comes back to you as a structured trip ticket.

The exact scope of your build is set in your written service agreement during setup.

3. AVA captures. Your dispatcher confirms.

AVA captures call details. It does not run your operation. Your dispatcher reviews every trip ticket, checks it against your availability and rates, assigns the vehicle and the chauffeur, and fulfills the run.

You stay responsible for rates, availability, vehicle assignment, chauffeur conduct, insurance, permits, and every trip you accept. Nothing AVA captures is a commitment by us to a caller on your behalf.
