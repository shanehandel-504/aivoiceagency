What it does

AI Chauffeur answers the phones for limousine and black car companies and books the trips. Dispatch slammed? It picks up every call. It takes the whole trip the way your best dispatcher would, quotes your rates and books it. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript.

On the call

  • Answers every call in your company name, around the clock.
  • Takes the whole trip: name and phone, date and time, pickup and drop-off, how many people, how many bags.
  • Airport runs: the airline and flight number, curbside or meet and greet at the terminal.
  • Picks the right car for the group. If there are more people than the car holds, it moves the trip up to a bigger car.
  • Child seats — which kind and how many.
  • Extra stops along the way.
  • Notes for the chauffeur: gate codes, coffee with cream and sugar, champagne on ice, whatever the customer asks for.

The quote

  • Quotes the trip live from your rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day, in your service area. A trip your rates don't cover goes to your dispatcher to quote.
  • Your rates are loaded for every vehicle you run, for trips in your service area.
  • Hourly minimums, late-night, same-day, and holiday rates are built in.
  • The customer hears one clean number, service fee included.

After the call

  • Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet: rate, vehicle, passengers and bags, every note for the chauffeur.
  • Trip sheet on your phone right after the call.
  • Your passenger is booked, with the booking on their phone.
  • With your CRM, it writes the trip straight into your reservation software.
  • Anything missing is flagged for dispatch, so nothing slips. Your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur.
  • Every call is recorded. The recording and the transcript come with every trip sheet.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

Does it quote the price on the call?
Yes. It quotes from your rate sheet, by vehicle and time of day, in your service area. The customer hears one clean number, service fee included.
What if the trip isn't on my rate sheet?
A trip your rates don't cover goes to your dispatcher to quote. Anything missing is flagged for dispatch, so nothing slips.
Where does the trip go after the call?
Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Your passenger is booked. Your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur. With your CRM, the trip is written straight into your software.
Is the call recorded?
Yes, every call is recorded. The recording and every word of the call come with the trip sheet.
