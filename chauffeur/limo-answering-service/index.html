Limo answering service · 24/7 reservations

The limo answering service that never puts a caller on hold.

Every call is answered the second it comes in. No queue, no hold, no voicemail. AI Chauffeur takes the whole trip, quotes your rates and books it. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript.

The problem

An unanswered call is a trip somebody else runs.

A fare who needs a car at 5 AM does not wait. They dial the next name on the map before your greeting finishes. There is no callback list for that call, because it was never a lead — it was a ride, and the ride left.

Three ways it happens, every week, in every operation that answers its own phone.

It rings out

The line is open and nobody is near it. The caller counts rings, decides you are closed, and hangs up. All you get is a number in the call log — no trip, no time, no idea what they wanted.

It rolls to voicemail

Voicemail is a form nobody wants to fill out. A caller who needs a black car this morning hangs up on the beep. The ones who do leave a message leave half a trip, and you spend the callback asking for the rest.

It lands on a busy line

Your dispatcher is already on a call. The second caller gets hold music or a busy signal. One person, one line, one conversation — and the third caller never gets that far.

How it answers

Ten callers at 5 AM. Ten calls answered.

AI Chauffeur is not one person with a headset and one line. There is no line to stand behind. Ten callers can dial your number in the same minute, and all ten are answered at once, on the first ring. No queue. No hold. No busy signal.

It works the same at 2 PM and 2 AM. Saturday night, a holiday morning, the middle of a snow event when every phone in the office is lit. The line answers airport runs, hourly charters, and black car pickups the same way at every hour.

And every call is taken all the way through. AI Chauffeur asks for pickup and dropoff, date and time, vehicle class, passenger and bag count. On an airport run it asks for the flight number too. Anything else the caller adds goes in the notes. Then it quotes from your rate sheet, reads the trip back and books it. Nothing is left for the dispatcher to go back and collect.

Two windows carry most of the weight. The one where the office is dark — after-hours limo dispatch — and the one where a flight time is on the line, which is airport transfer booking. Answering is the first part of a bigger job, and which part of that job a machine should be doing is the subject of limo dispatch automation.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

How fast does it answer?
On the first ring, at any hour. There is no queue in front of AI Chauffeur and no menu to sit through — the line picks up and the conversation starts.
What happens when three calls hit at once?
All three are answered at once. AI Chauffeur is not one person with one line, so the second and third callers are not stacked behind the first. Nobody hears hold music. Nobody hears a busy signal.
Does my dispatcher still assign the car?
Yes. AI Chauffeur books the trip on the call. Right after the call, your dispatcher has the trip sheet, the recording and the transcript. Your dispatcher assigns the car and the chauffeur. No car moves until your dispatcher sends it. Dispatch, changes and cancellations stay with your team.
What software does it work with?
It works with Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Moovs, Livery Coach, GNet, Hudson, SantaCruz (GroundWidgets), LimoExpress, A to Z Dispatch and RideBits. Nothing gets ripped out and nothing gets replaced. With your reservation software, the trip is written straight into your software. Where each platform stands is on the integrations page. The short answer is on works with your software.
How does setup work?
Our team sets it up for your company. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once we have your rates, your service area and who gets the trip sheet. We go over it on the setup call. Every step is written out in how setup works.
