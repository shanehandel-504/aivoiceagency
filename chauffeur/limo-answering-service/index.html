Limo answering service · 24/7 intake

The limo answering service that never puts a caller on hold.

Every call is answered the second it comes in — no queue, no hold, no voicemail. AVA takes the whole trip and hands your dispatcher a clean ticket to confirm.

The problem

An unanswered call is a trip somebody else runs.

A fare who needs a car at 5 AM does not wait. They dial the next name on the map before your greeting finishes. There is no callback list for that call, because it was never a lead — it was a ride, and the ride left.

Three ways it happens, every week, in every operation that answers its own phone.

It rings out

The line is open and nobody is near it. The caller counts rings, decides you are closed, and hangs up. All you get is a number in the call log — no trip, no time, no idea what they wanted.

It rolls to voicemail

Voicemail is a form nobody wants to fill out. A caller who needs a black car this morning hangs up on the beep. The ones who do leave a message leave half a trip, and you spend the callback asking for the rest.

It lands on a busy line

Your dispatcher is already on a call. The second caller gets hold music or a busy signal. One person, one line, one conversation — and the third caller never gets that far.

How it answers

Ten callers at 5 AM. Ten calls answered.

AVA is not one person with a headset and one line. There is no line to stand behind. Ten callers can dial your number in the same minute, and all ten are answered at once, on the first ring. No queue. No hold. No busy signal.

It works the same at 2 PM and 2 AM. Saturday night, a holiday morning, the middle of a snow event when every phone in the office is lit. The line answers airport runs, hourly charters, and black car pickups the same way at every hour.

And every call is taken all the way through. AVA asks for pickup and dropoff, date and time, vehicle class, passenger and bag count. On an airport run it asks for the flight number too. Anything else the caller adds goes in the notes. Nothing is left for the dispatcher to go back and collect.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

How fast does it answer?
On the first ring, at any hour. There is no queue in front of AVA and no menu to sit through — the line picks up and the conversation starts.
What happens when three calls hit at once?
All three are answered at once. AVA is not one person with one line, so the second and third callers are not stacked behind the first. Nobody hears hold music. Nobody hears a busy signal.
Does my dispatcher still confirm every trip?
Yes. Every single one. AVA captures the trip; your dispatcher confirms it. Nothing reaches a run sheet and no car moves until a human on your side says so. That is the whole design.
What software does it work with?
The call comes back as a clean trip ticket your dispatcher confirms in whatever you already run — Limo Anywhere, FASTTRAK, Santa Cruz, or a shared inbox. AVA captures the trip. It does not write into your dispatch software. Nothing gets ripped out and nothing gets replaced.
How does setup work?
Setup is founder-led and custom to your operation. Typical timeline is one to two weeks once your rate rules, service area, and dispatch process are documented. The build is scoped on the intro call.
