Setup · founder-led

How setup works.

No mystery and no long sales process. Here is every step between the intro call and AVA answering your line — and what we need from you to get there.

The build

Five steps, intro call to live line.

Setup is founder-led and custom. It is built by a thirty-year business owner, seventeen of those years running a chauffeured transportation company. Wisconsin-built, serving operators nationwide.

Typically one to two weeks, once your rules are written down. The order below does not change.

01

A twenty-minute intro call

What you run, how many cars, and what your phone does at 2 AM right now. No slide deck. By the end of twenty minutes we both know whether this fits your operation.

02

Your rules, written down

Rate rules, service area, vehicle classes, and how a trip reaches your dispatcher today. Most of it already lives in your head or on a rate sheet. This step just gets it on paper.

03

The build

AVA is written around those rules — the greeting, the questions it asks a caller, and every field on the trip ticket. Nothing generic. It asks for what your dispatcher needs to run the trip.

04

Test calls you listen to

You dial the line and give it a real trip. An airport run, an hourly charter, a Saturday night pickup. You hear every word. Wording gets changed until it sounds like your office, not ours.

05

Live on your line

You forward your line to AVA. Start with after hours only if you want, then move to all day once you have heard it work. Every call comes back as a trip ticket, and your dispatcher confirms every one.

One thing does not change at any step: AVA captures, your dispatcher confirms. AVA does not write into your dispatch software. It does not text or email the passenger. It does not assign a driver or send work out to an affiliate. All of that stays with your team, exactly where it sits now.

Your side of it

What we need from you.

Six things. You already have all six — most of them are in your head, and step 02 is simply getting them written down. None of this needs a project manager or an IT department.

  • Your rate rules or rate sheet. So AVA asks for the details your rate sheet needs, instead of a generic set of questions. Your dispatcher still prices the trip.
  • Your service area. So the ticket shows whether a pickup sits inside your area. Farm-out calls stay your team's decision.
  • Your vehicle classes. So the ticket names the car the way your run sheet names it — sedan, SUV, sprinter, coach.
  • How a trip reaches your dispatcher today. So the ticket fits the process you already run, instead of you bending around it.
  • Your after-hours arrangement. Who covers the phone tonight, and what should happen at 2 AM when nobody does.
  • A number to forward. The line your callers already dial. You keep it, and you keep control of the forward.

FAQ

Questions operators actually ask.

How long does it take?
Typically one to two weeks, from the intro call to a live line. The build itself is the fast part. What sets the pace is how quickly your rate rules, service area, and dispatch process get written down, and that part is mostly you.
What does it cost?
It depends on the operation, and that is not a dodge. Every build is custom to your rate rules, your service area, your vehicle classes, and the way your dispatcher works. There is no package to pick from, which is why there is no price list on this page. You get a real number on the intro call, before anything is built.
Do I have to change my phone number?
No. You keep the number your callers already dial. Setup is a forward from the line you have now, the same thing you already do when you send calls to an after-hours service. You control the forward and you can undo it.
What if it is not right for my operation?
Then you hear that on the intro call and you are out twenty minutes. Some operations do not need this. If your phone is covered at every hour and nothing gets missed, there is nothing here for you. We would rather say that than send a proposal.
Does AVA put the trip into my system for me?
No. AVA captures the trip and hands your dispatcher a clean ticket. AVA does not write into your dispatch software, text the passenger, or assign a driver. Your dispatcher confirms every trip. That does not change once you go live.
