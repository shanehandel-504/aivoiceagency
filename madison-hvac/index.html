// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

What Madison HVAC shops ask first.

[01]

Can AVA handle property managers differently from homeowners?

Yes. Known property-management numbers get recognized and routed by your account rules — multi-unit intake, PO capture, direct line to your service manager — while homeowner calls run the standard triage. You define the tiers at setup.

[02]

What happens during a real cold snap when calls stack up?

Cold-snap night is the design case: concurrency is scoped and tested to your call volume before launch, and AVA separates no-heat emergencies from morning-scheduling requests, and routes urgent dispatches to your on-call tech instead of dropping the caller to voicemail.

[03]

Which Dane County communities can you cover?

Your service map is the boundary: Madison proper, Middleton, Fitchburg, Verona, Sun Prairie, Waunakee, Monona, McFarland — AVA verifies the address on intake and screens out-of-area callers.

[04]

Does AVA integrate with our dispatch software?

Captured calls flow into the field-service platform you already run — job details, urgency tag, address, callback — so dispatch works from one queue instead of a voicemail box.

[05]

Can it book the August turnover inspections?

AVA captures unit lists, access instructions, and requested windows during turnover season and writes them into your schedule per your rules, so a forty-unit request does not evaporate because the office was already on a call.
