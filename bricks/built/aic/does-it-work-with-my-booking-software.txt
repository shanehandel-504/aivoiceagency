Yes. It connects to CRMs too. We check your reservation software and handle the connection.

Either way, every trip it books comes with a full trip sheet. The trip sheet lands in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript. Your team keeps working in the software it already runs.

Here is how a booking call feeds your team, step by step.

1. Set up for your company, it answers every call in your company name.
2. It takes the whole reservation: date, time, pickup, extra stops, drop-off, passengers, bags, flight number, child seats, gate codes, meet-and-greet or curbside, and notes for the chauffeur.
3. It keeps what the caller already said and asks only for what is missing.
4. It picks the vehicle from your own fleet list that fits the passengers and the bags.
5. It quotes from your rate sheet. A price that is not on the sheet is flagged for dispatch, never made up.
6. It reads the trip back, gets the caller's name and cell, and books it. The caller gets a text.

Answers the call. Quotes your rates. Books the trip. That part works the same whatever software you run.

What about the setup? It is built around your company: your company name, your fleet list and your rate sheet. Then we look at the software your team uses and handle the connection. You do not have to switch systems to use it.

What about calls on a trip that is already in your software? A change, a cancel, where's my car. It takes the message and texts dispatch right away. It never changes or cancels a trip itself.

What stays with your team: Your team handles dispatch, trip changes, cancellations and payment. Dispatch confirms the car and assigns the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch. Your software stays your software. The trip arrives complete, with the call behind it, so nothing has to be pieced together from a voicemail.
