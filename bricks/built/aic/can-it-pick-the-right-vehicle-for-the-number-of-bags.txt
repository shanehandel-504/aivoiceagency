BODY (300–450 words):
Yes. It picks the vehicle from your own fleet list that fits both the passengers and the bags. If nothing on the list fits, it does not guess. It flags the trip for dispatch.

Set up for your company, AI Chauffeur answers in your company name, takes the whole reservation and quotes from your rate sheet. Answers the call. Quotes your rates. Books the trip. Picking the vehicle is part of taking the reservation.

Here is how it works.

1. At setup, your fleet list goes in, sedan to 56-passenger coach. Those are your vehicles and your numbers, not ours.
2. A caller wants a trip. It keeps what the caller already said and asks for what is missing. Passengers and bags are asked on every new trip.
3. It looks at passengers and bags together. A party can fit the seats of a sedan and still not fit the trunk. An airport run with a lot of luggage is the usual case. The bags move the trip up to a vehicle on your list that holds them.
4. It quotes the trip from your rate sheet. A price that is not on the sheet is flagged for dispatch, never made up.
5. It reads the trip back and books it. The caller gets a text.

If no vehicle on your fleet list fits the passengers and the bags, the trip is flagged for dispatch. Your team decides what to do: a bigger vehicle, a second car, or a call back to the customer.

The trip sheet lands in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript. If the bag count looks off, dispatch can play the call and hear what the caller said.

One thing to keep straight. A vehicle that fits is not the same as a vehicle that is free. It knows what each vehicle on your list holds. Your team knows which cars are out that day.

What stays with your team: Your team handles dispatch, trip changes, cancellations and payment. Dispatch confirms the car and assigns the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch.

FAQ (3 pairs, one line each):
Q: Where do the passenger and bag limits come from? A: From your own fleet list, set up for your company.
Q: What if no vehicle fits the passengers and the bags? A: The trip is flagged for dispatch, and your team decides.
Q: Does it know which cars are free that day? A: No. It knows what fits. Dispatch confirms the car.
