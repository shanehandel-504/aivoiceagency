BODY (300–450 words):
Yes. Set up for your company, AI Chauffeur quotes from your own rate sheet, loaded at setup. A price that is not on the sheet is flagged for dispatch, never made up.

That is the middle beat of the whole product. Answers the call. Quotes your rates. Books the trip. A caller who gets a price on the first call has less reason to call the next company.

Here is how a call with a quote goes.

1. It picks up in your company name.
2. It takes the whole reservation: date, time, pickup, extra stops, drop-off, passengers, bags, flight number, child seats, gate codes, and meet-and-greet or curbside. It keeps what the caller already said and asks only for what is missing.
3. It picks the vehicle from your fleet list that fits the passengers and the bags.
4. It quotes the trip from your rate sheet, whether the trip is airport, hourly or point to point.
5. If the price for that trip is not on your rate sheet, it flags it for dispatch. It never guesses a number.
6. It reads the trip back and books it. The caller gets a text.

The rates are yours. Your rate sheet goes in at setup, and it quotes from that sheet. It does not invent a discount, a surcharge or a special.

The trip sheet lands in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript. If a customer ever says they were told a different price, the recording is there.

One note on the demo line. The demo does not quote a price. Set up for your company, it quotes your rates too.

What stays with your team: Your team handles dispatch, trip changes, cancellations and payment. Dispatch confirms the car and assigns the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch. A quoted price is part of the booking, and your team still runs the car, the chauffeur and the payment.
