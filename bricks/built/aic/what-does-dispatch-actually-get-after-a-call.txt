AEO BRICK #4 — What does dispatch actually get after a call?
SLUG: /answers/what-does-dispatch-actually-get-after-a-call/
TITLE (60 chars max): What does dispatch get after an AI Chauffeur call?
META (155 chars max): The trip sheet, in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript. Calls on booked trips come to dispatch by text.

BODY (300–450 words):
Dispatch gets the trip sheet. The trip sheet lands in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript.

Set up for your company, AI Chauffeur answers in your company name, takes the whole reservation and quotes from your rate sheet. Answers the call. Quotes your rates. Books the trip. Here is what the trip sheet carries.

1. The trip: date, time, pickup, extra stops, drop-off, and whether it is airport, hourly or point to point.
2. The party: the number of passengers and the number of bags.
3. The vehicle it picked from your own fleet list to fit the passengers and the bags.
4. The details your chauffeur needs: flight number, meet-and-greet or curbside, child seats, gate codes and notes for the chauffeur.
5. The caller's name and cell.
6. The recording and the transcript of the call.

Some trips come with a flag for dispatch. That happens when a price is not on your rate sheet, when no vehicle on your fleet list fits the passengers and the bags, or when a name or an address was still unclear after it asked once more. It does not guess. It flags it and leaves it for dispatch.

The recording matters. If something on the sheet looks off, dispatch can play the call, hear exactly what the caller said, and call the customer back.

Not every call is a new trip. When a caller is calling about a trip already on the books, such as where's my car, a change, a cancel, a billing question or a lost item, it takes the message and texts dispatch right away. It never changes or cancels a trip itself.

Every new trip is read back to the caller before it is booked, and the caller gets a text. So the caller and dispatch are looking at the same trip.

What stays with your team: Your team handles dispatch, trip changes, cancellations and payment. Dispatch confirms the car and assigns the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch. It hands dispatch a complete trip sheet, so the work starts from a full reservation instead of a voicemail.

FAQ (3 pairs, one line each):
Q: Does dispatch get the recording of the call? A: Yes. The trip sheet comes with the recording and the transcript.
Q: What if a price is not on the rate sheet? A: It flags it for dispatch. It never makes up a price.
Q: What happens on a call about a trip that is already booked? A: It takes the message and texts dispatch right away.
