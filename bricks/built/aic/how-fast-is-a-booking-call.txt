TITLE (60 chars max): How fast is a booking call with AI Chauffeur?
META (155 chars max): As fast as the caller. AI Chauffeur keeps what the caller says first, asks only for what is missing, quotes from your rate sheet and books the trip.

BODY (300–450 words):
A booking call is as fast as the caller. It keeps whatever the caller says first and asks only for what is still missing, so a caller who gives the whole trip up front is booked quickly.

Set up for your company, AI Chauffeur answers in your company name, takes the whole reservation and quotes from your rate sheet. Answers the call. Quotes your rates. Books the trip. Here is how a booking call runs.

1. It picks up in your company name.
2. The caller says what they need. Every detail the caller already gave is kept.
3. It asks only for what is missing: date, time, pickup, extra stops, drop-off, passengers, bags, flight number, child seats, gate codes, and meet-and-greet or curbside. A caller who already said two people, three bags, airport pickup is not asked again.
4. It picks the vehicle from your fleet list that fits the passengers and the bags.
5. It quotes from your rate sheet. A price that is not on the sheet is flagged for dispatch, so the call keeps moving.
6. If a name or an address is unclear, it asks once more. If it is still unclear, it flags it for dispatch instead of going around in circles with the caller.
7. It reads the trip back, gets the caller's name and cell, and books it. The caller gets a text.

The price is part of what makes it fast. The caller hears the quote on the same call, so there is no wait for a call back with a number.

After the call, the trip sheet lands in your CRM, your texts and your inbox, with the recording and the transcript.

Calls about a trip that is already booked are shorter still. Where's my car, a change, a cancel: it takes the message and texts dispatch right away. It never changes or cancels a trip itself.

What stays with your team: Your team handles dispatch, trip changes, cancellations and payment. Dispatch confirms the car and assigns the chauffeur. AI Chauffeur does not dispatch.

FAQ (3 pairs, one line each):
Q: Does it ask for details the caller already gave? A: No. It keeps what the caller said and asks only for what is missing.
Q: Does the caller get a price on the same call? A: Yes. It quotes from your rate sheet while the caller is on the phone.
Q: Does an unclear address slow the call down? A: It asks once more, and if it is still unclear it flags it for dispatch and moves on.
