// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

What Dane County practices ask us.

[01]

Is AVA giving out clinical or triage advice?

No. AVA records symptoms verbatim, applies the urgency rules your dentists define — swelling, trauma, uncontrolled bleeding escalate immediately — and otherwise schedules per your protocol. Diagnosis and advice remain with licensed staff, full stop.

[02]

Can AVA verify insurance on the call?

AVA captures carrier, member ID, and subscriber details exactly as your intake form specifies, so verification is queued for your team before the patient arrives. It does not promise coverage or quote benefits to callers.

[03]

We only need coverage at lunch and on Mondays. Possible?

Completely. Forwarding rules are yours: Monday mornings, the noon hour, after 5 PM, or overflow-only when the desk cannot answer by the third ring. Most practices start with overflow and expand once they see the capture log.

[04]

How do emergency toothache calls work after hours?

Callers describing what your protocol defines as urgent hear your on-call instructions and get captured for morning follow-up; everything else is offered the next open slot. Your practice manager reviews every after-hours encounter in the morning digest.

[05]

Will it sound like a call center?

No script-reading drone, no hold music maze. AVA answers with your practice's greeting in a natural voice and gets to the point — most callers just experience a phone that finally got answered.
