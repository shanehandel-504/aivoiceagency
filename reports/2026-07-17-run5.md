## Plain-English summary (for a non-coder)

The site had 3 Wisconsin city hubs (Milwaukee, Madison, Green Bay) and 15 city-trade pages,
but Green Bay and the new city Waukesha had no trade pages, West Bend had trade pages but no
"city" landing page, and there were no pages aimed at people literally Googling "answering
service for plumbers." This run filled all 16 of those gaps with brand-new pages, each written
from scratch for its own town and trade (no find-and-replace) — every one carries a sample
"captured call" ticket, the exact panic searches a homeowner types at 2 AM, and the same set of
honest, sourced after-hours facts. Four of the pages are aimed at business owners shopping for
phone coverage and pitch AVA at a flat $497/month against the $75–$1,725/month human answering
services. Everything is live, wired into the sitemap and the Green Bay hub, and passed every
content-law and rendering check. Nothing touched the homepage content or the demo phone line.

## Gotchas

- `git add -A` is a trap here: a stray untracked `sitemap.xml.bak-aichauffeur` + voice-stack
  backups sit in the tree. Staged everything explicitly (path list for source, `git add -u` for
  the stamp commit) so nothing untracked was swept in. `.gitignore` hardened as a backstop.
- The generator's `esc()` double-escaped `&rarr;`/`&times;` in the cross-link constants on the
  first pass (rendered as literal `&amp;rarr;`) — those fields are generator-controlled, so they
  must NOT be HTML-escaped. Fixed before commit.
- Vercel edge stagger: the first new URL 404'd for ~25s post-push, then 200. Normal propagation.
