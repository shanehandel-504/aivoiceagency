## Plain-English summary

The `/lsa` "3AM. Google Was Listening" ad-lander was live but **flying blind** — it had no
analytics on it at all. This run put eyes on it and turned it into the reusable **mold** every
future ad-lander gets cloned from, without touching a single word or color of Shane's page.

1. **`/lsa` can now be measured.** Every button tap and the first use of the loss calculator now
   report to Google Analytics — and our own traffic (bots, our devices, `?notrack=1`) is silently
   ignored so the numbers stay honest. Proven live in a real browser: the events fire for a real
   visitor and go completely silent under `?notrack=1`.
2. **`/lsa` reads better to Google.** Its structured data was upgraded to a proper Service listing
   that points at the page's own canonical URL.
3. **The page's background particle ring got a quiet tune-up** — it was secretly running two
   animation loops at once; now it runs one, pauses off-screen, and its reduced-motion "still frame"
   survives a phone address-bar resize. Nothing looks different; it's just lighter.
4. **A master template** (`templates/lander-master.html`) was carved out of `/lsa` — a documented,
   fill-in-the-blanks mold with 8 named slots. It's hidden from Google and not a real page.
5. **`/booked`** got a conversion polish: the "Add to Google Calendar" event now reminds the
   prospect they can hear AVA on the demo line first, and the 7-sample audio wall was re-confirmed
   playing.
6. The shared site footer's **"3AM. Google was listening → /lsa"** Insights link was propagated to
   every page (it was staged during the LSA run; this run shipped it site-wide).
