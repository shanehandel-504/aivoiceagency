## What changed, in one paragraph

The setup-call reminder workflow used to wake up every 5 minutes from 6:00 AM to 7:55 PM Central (168 runs a day). It now wakes every 15 minutes from 6:00 AM to 9:45 PM Central (64 runs a day). It still sends every reminder it sent before, once each, and nothing twice. The two short reminders, the owner's CALL IN 15 text and the AVA "starts in 15 minutes" text, now go out on the one quarter-hour tick between 5 and 20 minutes before the call. That is exactly 15 minutes before on the AVA calendar and on phone bookings. On the AI Chauffeur calendar, whose slots start at :00, :20 and :40, it is 10, 15 or 20 minutes before. The text carries the call time either way. When both calendars hold no live booking in the next 48 hours, each run stops after two calendar reads.

**(b) Coarser poll: every 15 minutes, on the quarter hour, 6:00 AM–9:45 PM CT.** Measured inputs:
- **Slot grids and hours:**
  - AI Chauffeur GHL calendar: 20-minute slots (:00/:20/:40), open 11:00–19:20.

  - Phone engine (WF-SHANE-SLOTS): :00/:30, 8:00 AM–7:00 PM.
  - Cal.com 7272016: hidden, last booking Oct 5.
- **Every reminder falls inside the window.** Given those hours, every reminder is due between about 6:30 AM and 7:15 PM CT.
- **Why 15 minutes is the widest tick:**
  - The 30-minute windows (T24, T9, T1) and the hour-long ones (R24, R9) take a 15-minute tick with room for one retry.
  - The two short windows (CALL IN 15, R15) were 9.5 minutes wide. They are now more than 5 and up to 20 minutes before the call, so exactly one quarter-hour tick lands in each, on every calendar grid.
  - A 20- or 30-minute tick would push the owner ping to 5 or 30-plus minutes before the call.

**The 48-hour skip:**
- The two GHL calendar reads now go first and look 48 hours ahead.
- New IF node `Any Booking?`: when both reads answer 200 and neither holds a booking that isn't cancelled, the run ends there. That means 5 nodes, no Cal.com, data-table or contact reads.

- The skip saves reads, not executions: n8n counts the run either way.

## Gate · event-driven (needs Shane in the GHL builder; not built in this run)

**Shape:**
1. A GHL webhook on booking or status change goes to a new n8n webhook.
2. n8n computes the reminder times.
3. Wait-until nodes hold to each reminder time.
4. n8n re-reads the appointment and dedupes.
5. n8n sends.

That is one execution per booking event, roughly 0–5 a day at today's volume, instead of 64.

**Order:** the next run builds the n8n webhook on a ZZ copy and prints its URL. Then:
1. GHL → Automation → Workflows → + Create Workflow → Start from Scratch.
2. Add New Trigger → **Customer Booked Appointment** → filter **In Calendar** = "AI Chauffeur Discovery Call" and "Discovery Call" → Save Trigger.
3. Add New Trigger → **Appointment Status** → the same two calendars → Save Trigger.
4. **+** → action **Webhook** → Method POST → URL = the n8n webhook from that run → Save.
5. Name it "ZZ → n8n reminders (appointments)" → Publish.

**Open question for that run:** whether these GHL triggers fire on API-created appointments (the phone engine creates them through the API). If they don't, WF-SHANE-SLOTS would post to the same webhook itself, n8n side.

## Gotchas

1. **The baseline was 168 a day, not 288.** The cron was already bounded to 6:00–7:55 PM CT on Oct 7. Oct 29 totalled 564 executions on the CT day (415 on the UTC day), not 332. 214 of those were the ZZ sink taking test-agent webhooks during QA runs.
2. **This change alone does not reach "under 40 a day".** The other two hourly pollers are 48 a day before any webhook. See step 5.
3. **The CALL IN 15 timing moves on AI Chauffeur calendar bookings.** It now lands 10, 15 or 20 minutes before the call, because that calendar starts on :00/:20/:40. It is exactly 15 on the AVA calendar and phone bookings. The text shows the call time.
4. **The two short reminders lose their in-window retry.** These are CALL IN 15 and the AVA 15-minute text. A failed send on their one tick is marked final, and the owner gets the failure email. Under v2.1 it was retried 5 minutes later.
5. **DST defect, already there before v3, not changed here.** On a clock-change day, the 9 AM touch is computed as midnight + 9 hours. This Sunday, Nov 1, that lands at 8:00 AM, and the 9 AM text is then held by the 9 AM–8 PM text rule (the sweep shows it). v2.1 did the same. No live booking is on Nov 1 today. The fix is one line per spot (`set({ hour: 9 })`) in a later run.
6. **The 48 h check reads the two GHL calendars, not Cal.com.** Cal.com 7272016 is hidden, has had no booking since Oct 5 and has no webhooks. A Cal.com booking whose GHL copy failed would not keep the run going.
7. **The auto-mode classifier held the live n8n PUT.** One AskUserQuestion approval cleared it.
8. **The new cron reaches 9:45 PM, as the PASTE set it.** Given today's calendar hours, nothing can be due after about 7:15 PM CT. A 6:00 AM–7:45 PM window would be 56 runs a day.

LESSON: Read the cron before quoting it: the floor for a poller is its shortest send window, so to cut the ticks, widen that window to the tick and prove it on every slot grid the calendars use.
