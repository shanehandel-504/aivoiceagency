**Date** 2026-08-06 · **Scope** `chauffeur/` + `tools/` only · **Host** aichauffeur.ai

## 1 · THE RELEASE BLOCKER, AND IT WAS BIGGER THAN THE BRIEF SAID

The brief named two sections. **It was all ten.**

`nav.top` is `position:fixed`, so it is out of flow and the scroll container
knows nothing about it. Every anchored section on the homepage landed its own
top edge at scroll offset 0 — under the bar. Measured at 390 before this run:

```
nav bottom 61px          section kicker top 56px
#demo #integrations #how #pain #features #crush
#lead-protection #setup #operators #faq          ALL TEN, at 390 AND at 430
```

Five pixels of every eyebrow rendered inside the header. GOOGLE LEAD PROTECTION
and Operator-built were simply the two a reader is most likely to reach from a
deep link, which is why they were the two that got noticed.

### The fix, and why it is two properties and not one

```css
:root{--nav-h:61px}                              /*  8 + 44 +  8 + 1 */
@media (min-width:1024px){:root{--nav-h:73px}}   /* 14 + 44 + 14 + 1 */
html{scroll-padding-top:var(--nav-h)}
section[id],main[id],.cb-form[id]{scroll-margin-top:16px}
```

**They ADD, and that was measured rather than assumed.** At 390: padding alone
settles the target at 61, margin alone at 16, the pair at **77**. Setting both
to the header height would have produced 122px of offset and looked like a
different bug. Splitting them is deliberate — `scroll-padding-top` clears the
**chrome** and lives on the scroll container, so it covers every target
including ones no rule names and applies to a link jump, a focus jump and
`scrollIntoView()` alike; `scroll-margin-top` is the **gutter** and belongs to
the target, so one target can opt out without anyone touching the clearance.

A private `scroll-margin-top:76px` on `.cb-form` existed in **two** files. Both
deleted rather than kept: from a later stylesheet either would have beaten the
shared rule at equal specificity and the total would have been 137px.

The rule lives in `assets/circulant.css` — the file all twelve pages load and
nothing else does. One copy, nothing to drift.

**`--nav-h` is asserted against `getBoundingClientRect()`** at all six viewports
on all twelve pages. A constant that describes the header is worthless the first
time the header changes and nobody re-measures.

### After

| | 390 | 430 | 1440 |
|---|---|---|---|
| section top | 77 | 77 | 89 |
| kicker top | 133 | 133 | 185 |
| nav bottom | 61 | 61 | 73 |

**11 anchors × 4 widths + 11 skip links: all clear.**

`<main id="main">` reads top 0 and that is geometry, not a defect: it starts at
document offset 0, so the scrollport cannot move above it. The assertion that
means something there is the user-visible one, and `.phead`'s own 96px top
padding delivers it.

## 2 · WHAT THE SIGNATURE COST TO GET RIGHT

§ 2 asked for an 8px chamfer on the upper-right corner **and** a neutral
exterior elevation **and** the site's press physics. Those three are in direct
conflict, and finding out cost one probe:

> **`clip-path` is applied AFTER `filter`, and it clips everything the element
> paints — its outline and its shadows included.**

Read as pixels on a 200×60 fixture over `--midnight`:

| | clip-path ON | clip-path OFF |
|---|---|---|
| outline at `offset:4px` | `7,11,20` — the page | `61,123,255` — the ring |
| `box-shadow` below the box | gone | present |
| `filter:drop-shadow` below the box | **gone** | present |

The third row is the one that matters and it is the one that reads backwards
from the spec prose: the filter runs *first*, so the shadow it produces lands
inside the region the clip then throws away.

A control that chamfers **itself** therefore has no elevation and **no focus
ring** — and a removed focus ring is banned outright. So the chamfer moved off
the host:

| | Carries |
|---|---|
| **host** — unclipped | the label, the elevation as a `drop-shadow`, and the sitewide `2px solid --signal-blue` focus ring, which survives because nothing clips it |
| **`::before`** — clipped, `z-index:-1` | the fill, the 1px border, both bevels, so all four follow the chamfer instead of ignoring it |

Re-read as pixels after the change: ring `61,123,255` at +4 outside the host,
drop-shadow present below it, fill `30,86,214`, top bevel row `63,118,228`,
bottom depth row `23,67,167`, top-right corner cut to background.

**The recipe.** `.btn-primary` · `.tel-btn` · `.demo-play-btn` only.
`--action-blue` fill, `#FFFFFF` label, `1px rgba(127,178,255,.45)`, radius 10px,
`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(127,178,255,.35)` top bevel, `inset 0 -2px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.22)`
bottom depth, `drop-shadow(0 8px 24px rgba(0,0,0,.35))` — **neutral black, no
hue**. Hover: `--action-blue-hover`, border `rgba(127,178,255,.60)`,
`translateY(-1px)`. Press: `#1A4CC2`, `translateY(2px) scale(.985)`, shadow
collapses to `0 2px 8px`. **Press 70ms, release 160ms**, `cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1)`.
Reduced motion: transforms and transitions off, colour states only, elevation
holds at its resting value.

`.rail-call` and `.nav-book` are **chrome** and keep button v2 — no chamfer, no
exterior elevation. `.btn-ghost` untouched.

### The gate this broke, repaired in the same commit

RUN 10's filled-CTA probe read only an element's own `background-color`. From
the moment the fill moved to a `::before`, that probe would have reported **one**
filled control on a fold carrying **two** — and reported it in green. It reads
both boxes now. *Moving a fill onto a pseudo-element blinds every probe that
reads `backgroundColor` on the element.*

## 3 · THE DEFECT THE RUN FOUND THAT NOBODY ASKED IT TO LOOK FOR

**Three filled "Book the setup call" primaries sat 520-670px apart on the
homepage**, in three consecutive sections: the Crush, GOOGLE LEAD PROTECTION,
and Setup. Any two adjacent ones share a viewport **at every width**.

`DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` § 3 has said *one primary per viewport* since RUN 10. RUN
10's probe measured the opening fold, because that is where the defect it was
written for lived, so this one was never seen.

| pair | gap at 360 | 390 | 430 | 768 | 1440 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crush → GLP | 640 | 606 | 579 | 606 | 668 |
| GLP → Setup | 581 | 520 | 520 | 334 | 316 |

Every one of those is under its own viewport height.

**Fixed by level, not by label.** The Crush and GOOGLE LEAD PROTECTION CTAs move
to the ratified **secondary**; string, target and `data-event` are unchanged.
The GLP copy law fixes that section's string and target, both untouched — it
says nothing about the control's level, and the level is what the rule governs.
The one filled ask below the hero now sits in "Setup, done for you", the section
whose subject *is* the call.

### And the probe that found it had to be rewritten too

The first cut stepped the page by half a viewport and reported two violations.
The analytic pass found the same page also failed at 768 in a **28px** scroll
window that no half-viewport step could ever land on. Two boxes A above B share
some viewport of height `h` **iff `B.top - A.bottom < h`** — exact, no step size
to get wrong. *A granularity that decides which defects exist is not a
measurement.* The live scroll sweep stayed, tightened to `h/3`, for the half
that genuinely cannot be computed from static rects: the rail's own arming.

## 4 · THE REST OF WHAT SHIPPED

### § 3 · The rail

Controls were already 56px and the safe-area inset was already in the padding.
One number was wrong: the bar measures **73px** (1 + 8 + 56 + 8) and
`body.rail-on` was paying back **72**. One pixel of the last line sat under the
rail's own top hairline for as long as the rail has existed.

The suppressor set is now read from the DOM rather than hand-tagged:
`[data-rail-hide]` plus `.btn-primary`, `.tel-btn`, `.demo-play-btn` and
`summary`. A filled primary added in a future run suppresses the bar without
anyone remembering to write an attribute, which is the failure mode a
hand-maintained list actually has. `summary` joins them so the bar is never
sitting on an FAQ control while a reader opens and closes rows.

### § 4 · The demo pair

**The pair the brief describes is on the homepage's `#demo` section, not on
`/demo/`** — `/demo/` carries no play control at all. The labels named in the
brief ("Play the call", "Run it yourself") match the homepage exactly, so that
is the pair that was fixed; the misattribution is recorded rather than guessed
around.

Both controls now take `.cta-stack`'s own geometry — column, 1rem gap, 520px cap
— because the hero already established it and a second idiom for the same job is
one idiom too many. Equal width, **56px each**, 16px gap. The first cut left the
ghost at 58 against the filled one's 56, because its content box is taller by
the arrow's line box; `padding-block:0` against `min-height` pins both.

### § 5 · The callback drawer

64px row, full width, and a chevron that rotates on expand. **The chevron
follows the RENDERED state, never `aria-expanded`** — that attribute is written
by JS from what CSS painted, so a rotation keyed to it points the wrong way for
one frame on every load and points the wrong way forever with scripting off. Its
selectors are the same ones that drive the collapse.

It is an inline SVG, not a text glyph: the two faces here are self-hosted
subsets and a metric-matched fallback silently steals characters the real face
does not carry.

**The module had a third state all along and painted it in neither colour nor
word.** Every failure left the header reading STANDING BY in `--neutral` while
the note underneath said the call had not been placed — and collapsed on a
phone the note is not even on screen, so the module's own header was the only
thing a reader could see and it was wrong. `● NOT SENT` in `--miss-red`
(5.26:1 on `--surface`) now covers every failure path, validation included, and
STATE LAW applies exactly as it does to the green: one synchronous block,
`transition:none` on the chip. The chip does not carry the reason; the one-line
explanation stays in the note, in prose. Touching either field clears it.

### § 6 · Containers

Open FAQ rows were outlined in blue on all four sides — the loudest object in
the section, saying nothing about which row it was. Border back to `--line`,
open state is a **3px inset rail** in `--signal-blue` down the left edge: it
marks position rather than perimeter, and an inset paints nothing outside the
box, so it stacks no elevation.

Radius cap 10px on every container over 12: `.console` 18 · `.crush` 18 ·
`.setup-card` 18 · `.ticket-card` 16 · `.calc` 16 · `.cal-shell` 16 ·
`.card--stage` / `#faq .card` 14 · `.step` 14 · `.feat` 14. **A pill is not a
container** — `.badge`, `.int-pill` and `.hc-state` keep their full round,
because a status chip at 10px reads as a small box rather than as a state; the
gate's test for "pill" is geometric, radius ≥ half the element's own height.
`.cal-shell` lives in `/book/`'s own `<style>`, which loads after `aic.css` and
therefore beats the shared block — a sweep that read only the two shared homes
would have missed it, and the first one did.

### § 6 · The measure, and the number the brief asked for

The brief asked for **~34ch**. That was not shipped, and the reason is stated
rather than quietly worked around: 34ch is roughly half the ratified measure and
would have broken `DESIGN-SYSTEM.md` § 9 and CLAUDE.md APPENDIX A from the other
side. There *was* a real defect underneath the ask — FAQ answers had no cap at
all and ran the full 1180px wrap on desktop, about **110 characters a line**
against a 60-75 band.

The first cut used `64ch` on the reasoning that it lands on the site's 560px
prose measure. Measured, it rendered 623.6px and **79-84 characters** — still
over. `1ch` is the advance of "0", and Space Grotesk's zero is markedly wider
than its average glyph, so a `ch` value overshoots real text by about a third
here. **`52ch` measures ~506px and 64-68 characters** — the same *character*
measure the 17px body gets from its 560px box. The measure was never 560 pixels;
560 was the number of pixels that produced 62 characters at 17px, and at 15.2px
the same count needs a narrower box.

### § 7 · Haptics

Feature-detected (`'vibrate' in navigator`), wrapped in `try/catch` because
Chrome throws outside a user gesture and a page that dies in a click handler
takes the click with it. **12ms** on a primary CTA tap, **`[20,40,20]`** on a
callback success only — fired inside the same block that flips the chip to
CALLING NOW. Never on a nav link, never on the drawer, never on the footer,
never on scroll, and **never on a failure**: a phone that buzzes when something
did not work teaches the reader to distrust the buzz. iPhone gets nothing by
design — the § 2 press physics are the tactile layer there, and on Android too.

### The one gate that did not pass clean

**Lighthouse performance, homepage: 97 → 95.** Stated as measured.

- Direct homepage A/B, five runs each way: **before 97/97/96/97/96, after 95/95/95/95/95.** Not harness spread — the after side is identical five times.
- **LCP 2497 → 2701ms.** CLS 0.000 both ways. TBT improved.
- The other eleven pages are **flat or better**: `how-setup-works` 96 → 97, the rest unchanged.

**The cause is exact, and the eleven other pages are the control that proves it.**
They carry the same 12,446 new bytes of CSS — in `aic.css` — and their FCP did not
move at all (1653 → 1652ms) and their score did not move. The homepage carries
its copy **inline in the document**:

| | before | after | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| `index.html` | 189,145 | 201,515 | **+12,370** |
| `assets/circulant.css` (all 12) | 19,006 | 21,471 | +2,465 |
| `assets/aic.css` (11 pages) | 94,422 | 106,868 | +12,446 |
| `assets/aic.js` | 21,830 | 27,342 | +5,512 |

≈ **14.8KB added to the homepage's critical path as document bytes**, against
14.9KB added to the other eleven as separately-fetched, cacheable stylesheet
bytes. Same rules, same comments, two different costs. **The homepage's inline
`<style>` is the cost centre, not the CSS.**

**And the lever is not comment density.** RUN 10 escalated that, and it is still
a real convention question, but this run's measurement points somewhere better:
the homepage is the only page that inlines the shared blocks, and it does so
because a trailing block has to win the cascade. **A `<link>` placed after the
inline `<style>` also comes later in the cascade** — so a shared trailing block
does not have to be duplicated into the document at all. That would remove this
class of cost permanently and collapse the THREE CSS HOMES trap with it.

It is not this run's change. Re-plumbing how twelve pages load their CSS on the
same day as an interaction pass is how a site breaks at 2AM. **Recorded as the
next run's first item, with the mechanism named.**

## 7 · GOTCHAS FOR THE NEXT RUN

1. **`clip-path` clips the outline and every shadow, and it runs AFTER
   `filter`.** An element that clips itself has no focus ring, no `box-shadow`
   and no `drop-shadow` either. Prove it with pixels; the spec prose reads the
   other way round.
2. **`scroll-padding-top` and `scroll-margin-top` ADD.** Split them — padding
   clears the chrome, margin is the gutter.
3. **A fragment jump is still travelling when a naive probe reads the box.**
   `scroll-behavior` is smooth here and its duration scales with distance, so
   hopping from the foot of the document to the hero outruns a 1s wait and
   reports a wildly negative offset that looks like a defect and is not. Measure
   anchors on a FRESH load, which is also what a deep link actually is.
4. **Moving a fill onto a pseudo-element blinds every probe that reads
   `backgroundColor` on the element.** Repair the probe in the same commit.
5. **A scroll step size decides which defects exist.** Where the question is
   geometric, answer it analytically. Half a viewport missed a 28px window.
6. **`ch` is not a character.** In Space Grotesk it overshoots real text by
   about a third. Cap a measure by measuring it.
7. **A page's own `<style>` beats a shared stylesheet.** A radius sweep that
   reads `aic.css` + `circulant.css` + the homepage misses the four page-local
   `<style>` blocks. `/book/` was the one that carried something.
8. **The homepage has no `<main>` and no skip link**, while the other eleven
   pages have both. Lighthouse a11y still reads 100, so no probe flags it. Not
   in this run's scope; recorded so the next one does not rediscover it.
9. **`.btn` drops to 12px with 0.03em tracking below 600px** — a leftover from
   when the label was upper-case. Legal against the 12px floor, weak on a 56px
   filled primary. Type sizing was outside an interaction-hardware pass.
