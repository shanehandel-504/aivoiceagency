## Plain-English summary (for a non-coder)

Two jobs, both **live on aivoiceagency.ai** and proven with real screenshots/pixels.

1. **The glowing "light" that travels around the AVA buttons was frozen on your desktop Chrome.** It looked fine on our test robots (headless), but on a real screen with a real graphics card it sat still. I found the exact reason — Chrome was caching the picture of the glow and never redrawing it — and rebuilt the glow so it *moves* the light instead of *redrawing* it. That trick works on every browser. I proved it by photographing the button edge twice, 0.8s apart, and counting the pixels that changed: **before = 0 (frozen), after = 879**. Live on your Dell's real Chrome now.

2. **Every "hub" page now shows a compact "sample call" pill** — the same proof from the homepage — that taps through to the homepage's live call theater, pre-set to the right trade. It's one shared component, so all 8 hub pages stay identical and can't drift. Nothing else on those pages changed.
