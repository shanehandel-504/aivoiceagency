// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions venues ask.

[01]

Can AVA take reservations directly?

Yes. AVA holds your reservation calendar — by venue, by date, by party size — and books direct into your reservation system or POS.

[02]

How does AVA handle event inquiries?

AVA captures full event details — date, guest count, type, budget tier, vendor needs — and routes the lead to your sales team or banquet manager with the file pre-populated.

[03]

Can AVA coordinate with vendors?

AVA captures incoming vendor calls — caterers, florists, AV, transportation — and routes them to your event ops contact with the relevant event context attached.

[04]

What about group bookings — twelve callers for a dinner?

AVA captures the group lead, confirms availability for the size, and either books direct or routes to a manager. No more "we can't fit twelve, sorry" losses.

[05]

Front desk overflow at 11pm — what happens?

AVA picks up while your desk staff handles a guest checking in. Captures the caller's reservation question, late arrival, or maintenance issue, and routes to the night auditor or on-duty manager.
