“Won't customers hate talking to AI?”

What callers actually hate is voicemail. AVA picks up in one ring, doesn't pretend to be a person, and gets straight to the point — what's wrong, how urgent, when can someone come. Not on day one — that's what setup is for. We start with call mapping: your services, your service area, your booking rules, what counts as an emergency, and where each kind of call should go.
