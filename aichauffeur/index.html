Operator Questions

The questions every operator asks.

Built by talking to dispatchers, not engineers. If your question isn't here, text us and it'll be added.

Will my callers know it's an AI?
Most won't. The voice is conversational, takes natural pauses, asks follow-up questions a senior dispatcher would ask. We train it on your brand voice and tone. If a caller does ask, the AI is honest — but in our pilots, fewer than 4% of callers asked, and those who did still completed their bookings.
What happens if it gets a booking wrong?
Two safeguards: (1) Every ticket lands in your queue for operator confirmation before it's locked — your dispatcher reviews quote, vehicle, and details before SMS goes to the caller. (2) The AI confirms back to the caller in plain English at the end of the call ("So that's a 6 PM pickup at LaGuardia, four passengers, Escalade, $485 all-in — sound right?"). Errors get caught at intake, not after dispatch.
How does it integrate with my dispatch software?
Three options: (1) Direct API integration if you use one of the major dispatch platforms (Limo Anywhere, Limo Live, Santa Cruz, ETS). (2) Email forwarding — tickets land in your dispatch inbox in your existing format. (3) Zapier/Make webhook to whatever you use. Setup typically takes one 30-minute call.
What about repeat customers and accounts?
The AI recognizes returning callers by phone number, pulls their account history, and adjusts the conversation accordingly — "Hey Marcus, picking you up at the usual?" type interactions. For corporate accounts with billing arrangements, the AI honors your pricing tiers and routes accordingly.
What if a caller is angry, confused, or has an unusual request?
The AI is trained to detect frustration, complexity, or out-of-scope requests and escalate. Three escalation paths: (1) Hot transfer to your on-call dispatcher's mobile. (2) Schedule a callback within a tight window. (3) Take a detailed message and SMS your team. You set the rules.
How long does setup take?
Pilot tier: same day. We forward your overflow line to our system in 30 minutes. Full deploy: 5-7 business days for custom voice training, integration build, and pricing rules calibration. Both tiers include a soft launch period where you can monitor every call before going fully live.
