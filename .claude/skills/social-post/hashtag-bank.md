# Hashtag Bank — AI Voice Agency

Referenced by `SKILL.md` § 4. **3–5 tags per post.** Composition:
**1–2 trade · 1–2 geo · at most 1 category.** Never more than five.

## Trade

`#plumbing` `#plumber` `#hvac` `#hvaccontractor` `#electrician` `#electricalcontractor`
`#roofing` `#roofingcontractor` `#dentaloffice` `#dentalpractice` `#limo` `#blackcarservice`
`#nemt` `#homeservices` `#tradesman` `#contractorlife` `#servicebusiness`

Match the trade in the post. A plumbing receipt does not carry `#hvac`.

## Geo

`#wisconsin` `#milwaukee` `#madisonwi` `#greenbay` `#waukesha` `#westbend` `#wisconsinbusiness`
`#milwaukeesmallbusiness`

Use the city the artifact actually came from. Wisconsin operators are the validation gate — geo tags
are how they find us, so they earn a slot on nearly every post.

## Category — pick at most one

`#missedcalls` `#answeringservice` `#aireceptionist` `#smallbusinessowner` `#afterhours`

## Banned

- `#ai` `#artificialintelligence` `#chatgpt` `#automation` `#tech` — pure reach tags. They deliver an
  audience that will never call a plumber, and they make the post read as bot output.
- Any tag unrelated to the post's actual content.
- Tag walls (6+). Reads as spam on every platform.
- Branded tags we do not own or have not established.
- Trending tags borrowed for reach. Same problem as reach tags, plus a credibility cost.

## Adding a tag

A tag joins the bank only after it appears in a post that actually performed, or after an operator
uses it themselves. Do not speculatively expand this list.
