# Social Post

Copy law is inherited from `/CLAUDE.md` — § 4 HARD LAWS, § 6 COPY SOURCE, § FORBIDDEN WORDS. This
skill covers the **shape of a post**. It does not relax a single copy law; social is where fabricated
proof is most tempting and most damaging.

Rendering the video/reel itself is the `ava-factory` skill's lane. This one is the words.

---

## 1 · PROVE-WORK LAW

**Show the work or don't make the claim.** This is the whole voice.

- Every post is anchored to a **real artifact**: a recorded call, a real transcript, a real timestamp,
  a real screen. If there is no artifact, there is no post.
- **Never invent** a number, a customer, a result, a review, or a "we helped X do Y."
- Sourced stats are the only stats. Current citable set: AgentZap 47% of calls outside 9–5 /
  73%-higher conversion · ServiceTitan 10–14.1% of HVAC calls after-hours · ~50→~3 shops answering
  2PM vs 2AM. **Cite the source in the post.** No unattributed percentages.
- Demo language only: **captured · routed · dispatcher will confirm**. Never "booked," "confirmed,"
  or "guaranteed" as a claim about the service (§ FORBIDDEN WORDS). Natural booking language is fine
  inside a clearly-labeled sample-call transcript, and only there.
- "Google says" = **verbatim quote only** (§ 6). Paraphrase is not attribution.
- AVA is "AVA." Never "she," never "her."
- Show the seams. A post that admits what AVA does *not* do outperforms one that claims everything,
  and it's the only version that survives an operator actually calling the line.

## 2 · CAPTION STRUCTURES

Pick one. Do not blend them — a caption that tries two shapes lands as neither.

### A · THE RECEIPT
> `2:47 AM. [trade] call, [city].` → what the caller said → what AVA did → what the owner woke up to.

The default. Concrete time, concrete trade, concrete outcome. No adjectives.

### B · THE MATH
> One line naming the loss → the arithmetic, shown → the number → what closes the gap.

Every input must be labeled as an assumption. Never present a modeled figure as a measured one.

### C · THE OBJECTION
> State the objection in the operator's own words → concede the true part → show the artifact that
> answers it.

Best-performing shape for skeptical trades. Requires a real artifact to land.

### D · THE TEARDOWN
> Play the miss → play the answer → point at the one difference that mattered.

Pairs with a call recording. Label both halves clearly.

### Mechanics
- **First line is the whole post.** It is the only line most people read — no windup, no "In today's
  video," no emoji ladder.
- Grade 5–7. Short sentences. Zero jargon (§ 4).
- Line breaks between beats. A wall of text does not get read on a phone.
- **One CTA, at the end.** Usually the phone number. Never three links.
- No emoji as bullets or icons (§ 3 bans emoji-as-icon; the same discipline applies to copy).
- Length: 2–5 short lines for a reel; up to ~120 words for a static/text post.

## 3 · PIN-COMMENT PATTERN

Every post gets a pinned first comment. It exists so the number is reachable without cluttering the
caption, and so the proof is one tap away.

```
Call AVA live right now — 414-240-8930
```

Optional second line, only when the post references a specific artifact:
```
Full call + transcript: aivoiceagency.ai/booked
```

### Rules
- **414-240-8930 only.** Never any other number, ever (§ PHONE). No personal or routing lines.
- Pin it immediately at publish. An unpinned comment is a dead CTA.
- Never put a claim in the pin comment. It is a doorway, not an argument.
- Same wording every time. Repetition is the point — the number should become the brand.

## 4 · HASHTAG BANK

Full list, per-platform counts, and the banned-tag rules: **`hashtag-bank.md`** (this skill's directory).

Short version: 3–5 tags, drawn from the trade set + the geo set + at most one category tag. Never a
reach-tag that has nothing to do with the post. Never more than 5 — a tag wall reads as a bot.

## 5 · PUBLISHING — Shane's hand, not yours

- Schedule **2×/day** in the **GHL Social Planner**.
- **Draft only.** Writing the caption, building the post, and queuing it is the job. **Publishing is a
  Shane action.** Never post, never send, never publish to a public surface without explicit approval
  in the conversation — approval on one post does not carry to the next.
- Deliver drafts as a copy-ready block: caption · pin comment · hashtags · artifact link.

## 6 · PRE-FLIGHT

- [ ] Anchored to a real artifact. Named.
- [ ] Zero fabricated numbers, customers, or outcomes. Sources cited.
- [ ] No "booked / confirmed / guaranteed / locked in" as a claim. No AVA she/her.
- [ ] "Google says" content is a verbatim quote, or the phrase isn't used.
- [ ] First line works alone.
- [ ] One CTA. Pin comment written. `414-240-8930` and nothing else.
- [ ] 3–5 hashtags from the bank.
- [ ] Grade 5–7. Read it out loud — if it sounds like marketing, rewrite it.
- [ ] Handed to Shane for publish. Not published.
