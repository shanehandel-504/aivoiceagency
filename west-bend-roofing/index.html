// 03 ── COMMON QUESTIONS

County roofing operations, answered.

[01]

Can AVA separate house, barn, and cottage calls?

Yes — structure type drives the intake: homes get insurance and shingle detail capture, agricultural buildings get dimensions and steel-versus-asphalt preference, lake properties get access and urgency handling. Your estimator sees a sorted list, not a jumble.

[02]

What happens to the insurance conversation?

AVA logs carrier, claim number, and adjuster timing as facts and schedules the inspection. It never predicts what insurance will cover — that conversation belongs to you, and callers are told exactly that.

[03]

We're a family shop. Who sees the captured calls?

Whoever you name: SMS to two phones, a shared job list, or your CRM if you run one. The kitchen-table office keeps working — it just stops missing the other twenty calls.

[04]

How wide can the storm coverage stretch?

As wide as you chase: West Bend, Barton, Addison, Wayne, Trenton, the lake districts, or across the county line when a cycle justifies it. You set the boundary per storm if you want — AVA screens to whatever the current map says.
