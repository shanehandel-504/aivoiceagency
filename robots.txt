# THE RULE THAT MAKES THIS FILE LONG, AND WHY IT IS NOT REDUNDANT:
# a crawler obeys exactly ONE group — the most specific User-agent match — and
# ignores every other group in the file, including `*`. So the moment a named
# group exists for a bot, that group must repeat the disallows, or that bot is
# told the internal cockpits are fair game. Every group below therefore carries
# the same two Disallow lines. Adding a bot without them is a leak, not a
# formatting choice.
#
# /work/ and /hq/ are the internal cockpits. They are also noindex; robots and
# meta have to agree, because a page blocked here can still be indexed from an
# external link and a Disallow prevents the crawler from ever reading the
# noindex that would have stopped it.
# ============================================================================

Allow: /
Disallow: /work/
