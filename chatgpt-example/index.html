How it works

01

The call comes in

A caller dials your business — a new lead, an existing customer, or an after-hours emergency. AVA picks up on the first ring, every time.

02

AVA handles it

AVA greets the caller, asks the right questions, captures the need, and books or routes the request — in a natural, on-brand conversation.

03

Your team gets the record

The moment the call ends, the details are written to your CRM and the right person gets an alert — with the context already attached.
