## The sheet, verbatim

```text
WHAT IT IS
AI Chauffeur is an AI phone agent for limo and black-car companies, built by AI Voice Agency. It's built for limo and black car first; the same system fits charter bus, shuttle, airport van, and other ground-transportation and logistics companies, scoped on the setup call. When it's set up for a company, it answers that company's line under the company name, 24 hours a day, one call or a thousand at once. A guarantee for a specific call volume is a team question. The site is aichauffeur.ai.

THREE THINGS, KEPT STRAIGHT
The demo: what the caller is on right now, the fast version, built to show a trip taken start to finish in about two minutes; it texts the trip sheet so the caller sees it a minute later. The demo skips rate quoting and software write-in on purpose, because they slow a demo down.
The base: everything under "what it does on a new trip," "calls about a booked trip," "owner alert," and "what dispatch gets, in the base."
Above the base: rate quoting on the call, writing the trip into the reservation software, CRM or API work, more lines, high volume. The product does these; they are sized to the company on the setup call.

WHAT IT DOES ON A NEW TRIP
Takes whatever the caller gives first and asks only for what's missing: date, time, pickup, stops, drop-off, passengers, bags, flight number for airport trips, child seat if needed, name, and cell. Picks the vehicle from the company's own fleet list using passengers and bags together, sedan to 56-passenger coach. Spells names and addresses back. Reads the whole trip back before the call ends. The caller gets a text: "Your information has been received. Dispatch will get with you." Dispatch gets the trip sheet by text and email within about a minute, with the call recording and the full transcript. Dispatch enters the trip, confirms the customer, and sends it to the driver, the same way they do now.

WHAT IT DOES ON CALLS ABOUT A TRIP THAT'S ALREADY BOOKED
A change, a cancellation, "where's my driver," billing, a lost item, a driver calling in: it takes the caller's name, cell, trip date, and what they need, tells them dispatch will get with them, and texts dispatch right away. It never changes, cancels, looks up, or gives a status on any trip.

OWNER ALERT (for a company's own line, once it's set up)
Included. The company turns it on or off. When a caller asks for a person, has a question the setup can't answer, or it's urgent, the agent can put the caller through live, send a text, or both, to a second number the company chooses. The caller hears "dispatch," never "owner."

REACHING THE TEAM (on this demo line)
The agent can try to connect a caller with the team live, 7 AM to 9 PM Central. If the team isn't available, the team calls back within a couple of hours during business hours, 8 AM to 6 PM Central, seven days a week, or the caller books the 20-minute setup call. The team is not taking vendor or sales calls.

WHAT DISPATCH GETS
The trip sheet, the call recording, and the full transcript after every new-trip call, delivered the way the company wants them. In the base: by text, by email, and on the company's own trip list (one row per call, open it any time), plus a client login with call history, transcripts, and billing. Above the base: straight into the company's reservation software.

WHAT STAYS WITH THE COMPANY
Confirming the customer, checking availability, changes, cancellations, trip status, dispatch, drivers, and payment.

PRICE
The base: $997 a month, $997 one-time setup, and 65 cents a minute of talk time. No contract; cancel any month. That's the price for a company of any size that wants its calls answered and the trip sheet sent to dispatch.
Above the base: writing the trip into the company's reservation software, CRM or API work, quoting the company's own rates on the call, more than one line, high call volume. Yes, it does all of that; the team just needs to look at the company's setup first, on the 20-minute setup call, and then gives one price fit to the company. A two-car operator and a 300-trip-a-day fleet both start with that same call.

SETUP
About seven days. The company provides: the company name as it should be said on the phone, the number that will forward to the line and when (24/7, after hours, or overflow), the fleet list with passengers and bags per vehicle, the dispatch cell and email for trip sheets, the owner-alert number and whether they want live transfer, a text, or both, and the name of their reservation software. Nine test calls before the line goes on: normal trip, bags forcing a bigger vehicle, caller hangs up halfway, unclear address, a change, a cancel, owner alert reached, owner alert not reached, and every call landing in the trip list.

THE COMPANY'S PHONE NUMBER
They keep it. It forwards to the line, or the line becomes their after-hours and overflow number.

LANGUAGES
Multi-language is an add-on, priced on order.

HOW TO TRY IT
This call. Book a trip like one of your customers would; you'll get the same text and the same trip sheet a company's dispatch gets.

WHO'S BEHIND IT
The founder spent thirty years running companies, seventeen of them in corporate ground transportation, and has built and sold several. Based in Wisconsin. Questions the facts sheet doesn't cover, partner and agency questions, and anything above the base go to the 20-minute setup call.

THE SETUP CALL
Twenty minutes with the team, any day of the week, booked on this call, never the same day. The caller gets a confirmation text.
```
